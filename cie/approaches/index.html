8 Assumptions

一页浓缩（syllabus p.16-19 verbatim）

考前 5 分钟过这一页。所有 assumption 文本均出自 CIE AS Psychology Syllabus 2024-2026（634461）p.16-19 原文，可直接抄进答卷。

🧠 Biological Approach（syllabus p.16）

  • A1（p.16）：Behaviour, cognitions and emotions can be explained in terms of the working of the brain and the effect of hormones, genetics and evolution.
  • A2（p.16）：Similarities and differences between people can be understood in terms of biological factors and their interaction with other factors.

💭 Cognitive Approach（syllabus p.17）

  • A1（p.17）：Information is processed through the same route in all humans: input – process – output, in a similar way to how information is processed by a computer.
  • A2（p.17）：There are individual differences in cognitive processes such as attention, language, thinking and memory. These processes can also help to explain behaviour and emotion.

👥 Social Approach（syllabus p.19）

  • A1（p.19）：Behaviour, cognitions and emotions are influenced by social contexts, social environments and groups.
  • A2（p.19）：Behaviour, cognitions and emotions can be influenced by the actual, implied or imagined presence of others.

🎓 Learning Approach（syllabus p.18）

  • A1（p.18）：We all begin life as a blank slate. Experiences and interactions with the environment shape our behaviour and these changes are directly observable.
  • A2（p.18）：We learn through the processes of operant conditioning, classical conditioning and social learning. This can be understood using the stimulus-response model.

🧠

Biological Approach / 生物取向

Brain · Hormones · Genetics · Evolution — 3 core studies: Holzel · Dement · Hassett

📘 Syllabus p.16 · Paper 2 Q4(a) · Paper 1 I&D
A1

Brain, hormones, genetics & evolution explain behaviour

Syllabus p.16 verbatim
Behaviour, cognitions and emotions can be explained in terms of the working of the brain and the effect of hormones, genetics and evolution.

💡 关键概念 echo（学生答 Q4(a) 模板）：必须命中 syllabus 关键词 brain、hormones、genetics、evolution 至少 2 个，example 必须连回其中之一。

Holzel et al. (2011) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Holzel et al. (2011) found that participants who completed an 8-week mindfulness (MBSR) programme showed increased grey matter density in the hippocampus — a brain structure linked to memory and emotional regulation. This shows that the working of the brain changed, so a biological factor explains a change in cognition and emotion.

例：Holzel 等人(2011)发现，完成 8 周正念减压课程(MBSR)的参与者，海马回——一个与记忆和情绪调节有关的脑结构——灰质密度增加。这是「大脑的工作」发生了改变，因此生物因素可以解释认知与情绪的变化。

→ 看完整 study
Dement & Kleitman (1957)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Dement and Kleitman (1957) found that participants woken during REM sleep — a stage identified by a distinctive brain-wave pattern on the EEG — almost always reported a vivid dream, whereas those woken in non-REM sleep rarely did. This directly links a cognition (dreaming) to the working of the brain (REM brain-wave activity).

例：Dement 与 Kleitman (1957) 发现，在 REM 睡眠阶段（由 EEG 上特征性的脑波模式识别）被唤醒的参与者几乎总能报告出生动的梦境，而在非 REM 阶段被唤醒者很少报告。这把认知（做梦）直接与「大脑的工作」（REM 脑波活动）联系起来。

→ 看完整 study
Hassett et al. (2008)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Hassett et al. (2008) found that male rhesus monkeys spent more time with wheeled toys and females with plush toys — the same sex-typed pattern shown by human children. Because these monkeys were not exposed to human gender socialisation, the difference is best explained by evolution.

例：Hassett 等人(2008)发现，雄性恒河猴更多玩轮子类玩具，雌性更多玩毛绒玩具——与人类儿童相同的性别化模式。由于这些恒河猴未受到人类性别社会化的影响，该差异最适合用进化来解释。

→ 看完整 study
⭐ Q4(a) 完整 3 分作答示范 = AO1（上方 verbatim）+ AO2（★Best fit 卡片）拼装 AO1×2 AO2×1
One assumption of the biological approach is that behaviour, cognitions and emotions can be explained in terms of the working of the brain and the effect of hormones, genetics and evolution (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, Holzel et al. (2011) used MRI scans and found that participants who completed an 8-week mindfulness programme showed increased grey matter density in the brain's hippocampus (a brain structure linked to memory and emotional regulation) (AO2 = 1 mark, example directly linked to the brain keyword). 生物取向的一个假设是，行为、认知和情绪可以通过大脑的工作以及激素、基因和进化的影响来解释。例：Holzel 等人(2011)用 MRI 扫描发现，完成 8 周正念课程的参与者大脑海马回（与记忆和情绪调节相关的脑区）灰质密度增加。
⚠️ MS 反面教材警示："Do not credit answers that describe the aim or procedure, rather than a finding linked to the assumption." If you write "Holzel used MRI scanners", that is procedure, not a finding linked to the assumption. Must be finding (e.g., "increased grey matter") + keyword (e.g., "brain structure").

A2

Individual differences via biological factors + interactions

Syllabus p.16 verbatim
Similarities and differences between people can be understood in terms of biological factors and their interaction with other factors.

💡 关键概念 echo：命中 biological factors + interaction with other factors。 Example 必须显示"both similarities AND differences" 或 "interaction"。

Hassett et al. (2008) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Hassett et al. (2008) found that male rhesus monkeys showed the same preference for wheeled toys that human boys do — a similarity explained by biological factors — but the monkeys' preference was weaker than the children's, and this difference suggests that biological factors interact with other factors such as social experience.

例：Hassett 等人(2008)发现，雄性恒河猴与人类男孩一样偏好轮子类玩具——这是由生物因素解释的相似性；但猴子的偏好弱于儿童，这一差异说明生物因素还会与其他因素（如社会经验）相互作用。

→ 看完整 study
Holzel et al. (2011)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Holzel et al. (2011) found that all participants in the MBSR group showed an increase in grey matter — a similarity explained by biological factors — but the amount of increase varied considerably from person to person, showing biological factors interacting with other factors (individual differences).

例：Holzel 等人(2011)发现 MBSR 组的参与者都出现了灰质增加——由生物因素解释的相似性；但增加幅度因人而异，说明生物因素与其他因素（个体差异）相互作用。

→ 看完整 study
Dement & Kleitman (1957)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Dement and Kleitman (1957) found that all participants showed the same pattern of REM / non-REM brain activity — a similarity — yet participants differed consistently in how often they recalled their dreams. That difference cannot be explained by brain activity alone, so biological factors must interact with other factors.

例：Dement 与 Kleitman (1957) 发现所有参与者都表现出相同的 REM／非 REM 脑活动模式——这是相似性；但在回忆梦境的频率上存在稳定的个体差异。该差异无法单靠脑活动解释，说明生物因素必须与其他因素相互作用。

→ 看完整 study
⭐ Q4(a) 完整 3 分作答示范 = AO1（上方 verbatim）+ AO2（★Best fit 卡片）拼装 AO1×2 AO2×1
Another assumption is that similarities and differences between people can be understood in terms of biological factors and their interaction with other factors (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, Hassett et al. (2008) found that male rhesus monkeys showed the same preference for wheeled toys that human boys do (a similarity explained by biological factors), but monkeys showed a weaker preference than human children, suggesting that biological factors interact with social/cultural factors (AO2 = 1 mark). 另一个假设是，人与人之间的相似性和差异可以通过生物因素及其与其他因素的相互作用来理解。例：Hassett (2008) 发现雄性恒河猴和人类男孩一样偏好轮子类玩具（生物因素解释的相似性），但猴子的偏好弱于儿童，说明生物因素还与社会/文化因素相互作用。
⚠️ MS 警示：这一题最容易"抓瞎"——很多学生把 example 写成"monkeys prefer wheeled toys"（只说 difference），缺了 similarity 维度。MS 会 credit partial，但只给 1/2 marks。必须 example 同时 echo "similarities AND differences"。

📝 Exam Practice — Bio A2

Q4(a) · 3 marks · AO1 ×2 + AO2 ×1
"Outline one assumption of the biological approach, including an example from the study by Holzel et al. (2011)."
AO1 (2 marks): A second assumption of the biological approach is that similarities and differences between people can be understood in terms of biological factors and their interaction with other factors.

AO2 (1 mark): Holzel found that all MBSR participants showed grey matter increase (similarity due to biological factors), but the amount of increase varied considerably between participants (individual differences caused by the interaction between training experience and individual brain biology).

Cognitive Approach / 认知取向

Information Processing · Mental Processes — 3 core studies: Andrade · Baron-Cohen · Pozzulo

📘 Syllabus p.17 · Paper 2 Q4(a) · Paper 1 I&D
A1

Information processed input–process–output (computer model)

Syllabus p.17 verbatim
Information is processed through the same route in all humans: input – process – output, in a similar way to how information is processed by a computer.

💡 关键概念 echo：input – process – output 三段式 + computer metaphor 必须命中。

Andrade (2010) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Andrade (2010) found that participants who doodled while listening to a dull telephone message recalled 29% more information (mean 7.5 items) than the non-doodling control group (mean 5.8 items). The message was the input, doodling helped maintain attention during processing, and the recalled facts were the output — the same route in every participant, just as a computer processes information.

例：Andrade (2010) 发现，边听单调电话留言边涂鸦的参与者比对照组多回忆 29% 的信息（平均 7.5 vs 5.8 项）。留言是 input，涂鸦帮助维持注意以完成 processing，回忆出的内容就是 output。

→ 看完整 study
Baron-Cohen et al. (2001)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, in Baron-Cohen et al.'s (2001) Eyes Test every participant received the same visual input — a photograph of the eye region — then had to process it by inferring the mental state being shown, and finally produced a verbal output by choosing one of four words. The autism groups and the control groups all followed this identical route.

例：Baron-Cohen 等人(2001)的眼测试中，每位参与者先接收相同的视觉 input（眼部照片），再通过推断所表达的心理状态完成 processing，最后从四个词中选出一个作为 output。自闭症组与对照组都走这条相同路径。

→ 看完整 study
Pozzulo et al. (2011)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, in Pozzulo et al. (2011) every participant — child or adult — watched the same four 6-second video clips (input), encoded and retrieved the face from memory (processing), and then pointed either to a photograph or to a blank silhouette (output). All 112 participants went through the identical route, regardless of age.

例：Pozzulo 等人(2011)中，所有参与者（无论儿童还是成人）都观看相同的四段 6 秒影片（input），编码并检索面孔记忆（processing），再指认照片或指向空白剪影（output）。112 位参与者走的是同一条路径。

→ 看完整 study
⭐ Q4(a) 完整 3 分作答示范 = AO1（上方 verbatim）+ AO2（★Best fit 卡片）拼装 AO1×2 AO2×1
One assumption of the cognitive approach is that information is processed through the same route in all humans: input – process – output, in a similar way to how information is processed by a computer (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, Andrade (2010) demonstrated this when participants in the doodling condition simultaneously processed two streams of information (monitoring task input + doodling) and produced output (better recall), similar to how a computer runs parallel processes (AO2 = 1 mark). 认知取向的一个假设是，信息在所有人类中通过相同的路径处理：输入—处理—输出，类似于计算机处理信息的方式。例：Andrade (2010) 中涂鸦组同时处理两个信息流（监控任务输入+涂鸦），产生输出（更好的回忆）。
⚠️ MS 警示："input – process – output" 这个短语必须 verbatim（连字符 + 顺序）。不能在 example 里只说"two tasks"，缺 input/process/output 三段式。Examiner 会 credit 第一段但扣第二段。

📝 Exam Practice — Cog A1

Q4(a) · 3 marks · AO1 ×2 + AO2 ×1
"Outline one assumption of the cognitive approach, including an example from the study by Baron-Cohen et al. (2001)."
AO1 (2 marks): Information is processed through the same route in all humans: input – process – output, in a similar way to how information is processed by a computer.

AO2 (1 mark): Baron-Cohen's Eyes Test required participants to receive visual input (photographs of eyes), mentally process the information to identify emotions, and produce a verbal output by selecting one of four words — demonstrating the input-process-output route.
A2

Individual differences in cognitive processes (attention / language / thinking / memory)

Syllabus p.17 verbatim
There are individual differences in cognitive processes such as attention, language, thinking and memory. These processes can also help to explain behaviour and emotion.

💡 关键概念 echo：individual differences + 至少 1 个 cognitive process（attention/language/thinking/memory）+ "explain behaviour and emotion"。

Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) found a sex difference on the Eyes Test (males scored lower than females, though some of these differences were not significant), and that adults with autism or Asperger syndrome scored significantly lower than the control groups. This shows individual differences in a cognitive process — theory of mind (thinking about other people's mental states) — which helps explain group differences in social behaviour and emotion.

例：Baron-Cohen 等人(2001)发现眼测试存在性别差异（男性低于女性，但部分差异未达显著），且自闭症／阿斯伯格成人得分显著低于对照组。这显示了认知过程——心智理论（思考他人心理状态）——的个体差异，可帮助解释社会行为与情绪上的群体差异。

→ 看完整 study
Pozzulo et al. (2011)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Pozzulo et al. (2011) found that children aged 4–7 identified familiar cartoon faces as accurately as adults (their memory was intact), yet they were significantly worse than adults at correctly rejecting a target-absent humans line-up. This individual difference in how memory is used to make a decision helps explain age differences in eyewitness behaviour.

例：Pozzulo 等人(2011)发现 4–7 岁儿童识别熟悉的卡通面孔与成人同样准确（记忆完好），但在「目标不存在列队」中正确拒认的能力显著低于成人。这一「记忆如何用于决策」的个体差异，可解释目击者行为上的年龄差异。

→ 看完整 study
Andrade (2010)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Andrade (2010) found that the doodling group recalled 29% more than the control group, and that the control group's poorer performance was linked to lapsed attention (daydreaming). The difference in attention — a cognitive process — explains the difference in recall behaviour between the two groups.

例：Andrade (2010) 发现涂鸦组比对照组多回忆 29%，且对照组的较差表现与注意涣散（做白日梦）有关。注意这一认知过程的差异，解释了组间回忆行为的差异。

→ 看完整 study
⭐ Q4(a) 完整 3 分作答示范 = AO1（上方 verbatim）+ AO2（★Best fit 卡片）拼装 AO1×2 AO2×1
A second assumption of the cognitive approach is that there are individual differences in cognitive processes such as attention, language, thinking and memory, and these processes can also help to explain behaviour and emotion (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) found that males scored lower than females on the Eyes Test (a sex difference in a cognitive process), demonstrating individual differences in theory of mind (a cognitive process), and this cognitive difference helps explain differing social-emotional behaviours between males and females (AO2 = 1 mark). 另一个假设是认知过程（如注意、语言、思维和记忆）存在个体差异，这些过程也可以帮助解释行为和情绪。例：Baron-Cohen 等人(2001) 发现女性在眼测试中得分显著高于男性，说明心智理论的个体差异。
⚠️ MS 警示：写 assumption 时必须 4 个 process 都用 syllabus 顺序（attention → language → thinking → memory）。Examiner 普遍接受少 1-2 个，但必须含 attention 或 memory 一个，因为这两个最常考。

📝 Exam Practice — Cog A2

Q4(a) · 3 marks · AO1 ×2 + AO2 ×1
"Outline one assumption of the cognitive approach, including an example from the study by Pozzulo et al. (2011)."
AO1 (2 marks): A second assumption is that there are individual differences in cognitive processes such as attention, language, thinking and memory. These processes can also help to explain behaviour and emotion.

AO2 (1 mark): Pozzulo found that older children made significantly more correct identifications than younger children — showing individual differences in memory processes, which helps explain age-related differences in behaviour (eyewitness accuracy).

Social Approach / 社会取向

Social Influence · Presence of Others — 3 core studies: Milgram · Piliavin · Perry

📘 Syllabus p.19 · Paper 2 Q4(a) · Paper 1 I&D
A1

Social contexts, environments & groups shape behaviour

Syllabus p.19 verbatim
Behaviour, cognitions and emotions are influenced by social contexts, social environments and groups.

💡 关键概念 echo：social contexts / environments / groups 三者至少 1 个 verbatim 出现。

Milgram (1963) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Milgram (1963) found that 65% of participants (26/40) obeyed fully to 450V when the study was carried out in the prestigious Yale University laboratory, a setting that gave the procedure legitimacy. In Milgram's later Bridgeport variation, run in a run-down office building rather than a university laboratory, obedience fell to 47.5% — the social context alone changed how far participants would go.

例：Milgram (1963) 发现在声望卓著的耶鲁大学实验室中，65%（26/40）的参与者完全服从到 450V；后来在 Bridgeport 变体中把同一程序移到一栋破旧办公楼（而非大学实验室）时，服从率降至 47.5%——仅社会情境的改变就改变了行为。

→ 看完整 study
Piliavin et al. (1969)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Piliavin et al. (1969) carried out the study in the social environment of a New York subway carriage — a public, confined space that passengers could not leave, on a train running non-stop between stations. In this environment victims were helped very often: the cane victim in 95% of trials (62/65) and the drunk victim in 50% (19/38), with an overall helping rate of 78%.

例：Piliavin 等人(1969)在纽约地铁车厢这一社会环境中进行——公共、封闭、乘客无法离开，列车在站间不停。在该环境中受害者受助率很高：手杖受害者 95%（62/65），醉酒受害者 50%（19/38），总体帮助率 78%。

→ 看完整 study
Perry et al. (2015)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Perry et al. (2015) found that who the approaching figure was strongly shaped preferred distance: participants kept the greatest distance from a stranger (39.82%), then an authority figure (34.12%), then the neutral ball (20.20%), and the least from a friend (12.46%). The social context of the encounter determined the behaviour.

例：Perry 等人(2015)发现，靠近的对象是谁强烈影响偏好距离：对陌生人留出最大距离(39.82%)，其次权威人物(34.12%)，再是中性的球(20.20%)，对朋友最小(12.46%)。相遇的社会情境决定了行为。

→ 看完整 study
⭐ Q4(a) 完整 3 分作答示范 = AO1（上方 verbatim）+ AO2（★Best fit 卡片）拼装 AO1×2 AO2×1
One assumption of the social approach is that behaviour, cognitions and emotions are influenced by social contexts, social environments and groups (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, in Milgram's (1963) study, participants were significantly more likely to obey the experimenter in the prestigious Yale University laboratory (a high-status social context) than in a run-down office building — showing that the social context directly shaped participants' obedient behaviour (AO2 = 1 mark). 社会取向的一个假设是行为、认知和情绪受社会情境、社会环境和群体的影响。例：Milgram (1963) 在耶鲁大学实验室比在破旧办公楼中表现出更高的服从率。
⚠️ MS 警示："social" 是关键词。写 example 时必须 verbatim 用 "social context" / "social environment"，否则 example 不算 link 回 assumption。例如写"in a lab"不够，要写"the social context of a prestigious institution"。

📝 Exam Practice — Soc A1

Q4(a) · 3 marks · AO1 ×2 + AO2 ×1
"Outline one assumption of the social approach, including an example from the study by Piliavin et al. (1969)."
AO1 (2 marks): Behaviour, cognitions and emotions are influenced by social contexts, social environments and groups.

AO2 (1 mark): Piliavin conducted the study in a social environment — the subway carriage was a confined, public space in which passengers could directly see each other and the victim, and in this real-world context a spontaneous helping rate of 78% was recorded (far higher than the 30–40% typical of laboratory studies), with larger groups actually helping faster (median 1.5 seconds when 7+ males were present). The social environment directly shaped helping behaviour.
A2

Actual, implied or imagined presence of others influences behaviour

Syllabus p.19 verbatim
Behaviour, cognitions and emotions can be influenced by the actual, implied or imagined presence of others.

Blank slate — environment shapes behaviour

Syllabus p.18 verbatim
We all begin life as a blank slate. Experiences and interactions with the environment shape our behaviour and these changes are directly observable.

💡 关键概念 echo：blank slate + experiences + environment shape behaviour + "directly observable"。

Bandura et al. (1961) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Bandura et al. (1961) found that children who observed an aggressive adult model later imitated the model's specific aggressive acts — for instance hitting the Bobo doll with a mallet — which they had not shown before the model appeared. The new aggressive behaviour was directly observable in the test phase, showing it was shaped by environmental experience rather than being innate.

例：Bandura 等人(1961)发现，观察过攻击性成人榜样的儿童随后模仿了榜样的具体攻击行为（如用木槌打 Bobo doll），而这些行为在榜样出现前并未出现。新的攻击行为在测试阶段可直接观察到，说明它由环境经验塑造而非天生。

For example, Saavedra and Silverman (2002) treated a 9-year-old boy whose phobia of buttons was not innate — he was a blank slate at birth, and the fear was learned at age five when a whole bowl of buttons tipped over him during a classroom art project. A2

Learning through conditioning & social learning (S-R model)

Syllabus p.18 verbatim
We learn through the processes of operant conditioning, classical conditioning and social learning. This can be understood using the stimulus-response model.

💡 关键概念 echo：operant conditioning + classical conditioning + social learning 三者必须都出现 + stimulus-response。

Saavedra & Silverman (2002) ★ Best fit
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, in Saavedra and Silverman (2002) the boy's button phobia was acquired through classical conditioning: the previously neutral stimulus (buttons) was paired with an unconditioned stimulus (the distressing experience of a whole bowl of buttons falling onto him), which produced a conditioned response (disgust and fear). The stimulus–response link became strong enough to disrupt his daily life.

例：Saavedra & Silverman (2002) 中男孩的纽扣恐惧通过经典条件反射获得：原本中性的刺激（纽扣）与无条件刺激（整碗纽扣洒在身上的痛苦经历）配对，产生了条件反应（厌恶与恐惧）。这一刺激—反应联结强到足以影响他的日常生活。

→ 看完整 study
Bandura et al. (1961)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Bandura et al. (1961) demonstrated social learning: children who observed an aggressive adult model later produced imitative aggressive behaviours (e.g. hitting the Bobo doll with a mallet) that they had not shown before, even though no direct reinforcement was given to the child. The modelled act functioned as the stimulus and the copied act as the response.

例：Bandura 等人(1961)示范了社会学习：观察过攻击性成人的儿童随后做出了模仿性的攻击行为（如用木槌打 Bobo doll），而这些行为此前并未出现，且儿童本人未获得任何直接强化。被示范的行为充当 stimulus，被模仿的行为即 response。

→ 看完整 study
Fagen et al. (2014)
AO2 完整作答 · 1 mark

For example, Fagen et al. (2014) used operant conditioning: each step of the trunk-wash chain was shaped through successive approximations, with every correct response to the trainer's cue (the stimulus) marked by a whistle and reinforced with chopped banana. All four juvenile elephants learned the full sequence, with 'trunk here' the hardest step (~295 offers needed).

例：Fagen 等人(2014)运用操作条件反射：冲洗序列的每一步都通过渐进趋近法塑形，每次对训练者指令（stimulus）做出正确反应（response）就以哨声标记并给予香蕉强化。四头幼象都学会了完整序列，其中「放象鼻」最难（约需 295 次）。

→ 看完整 study

A second assumption of the learning approach is that we learn through the processes of operant conditioning, classical conditioning and social learning, and this can be understood using the stimulus-response model (AO1 = 2 marks). For example, Saavedra and Silverman (2002) used a form of classical conditioning (evaluative conditioning): the neutral stimulus (buttons) was paired with the unconditioned stimulus (a whole bowl of buttons tipping over him during a classroom art project at age five), producing a conditioned response (fear/avoidance of buttons) — directly demonstrating the stimulus-response learning process (AO2 = 1 mark). 另一个假设是学习通过操作条件、经典条件反射和社会学习进行，这可以用刺激-反应模型理解。例：Saavedra & Silverman (2002) 经典条件反射——中性刺激（纽扣）与无条件刺激（5 岁时课堂上整碗纽扣洒在身上的痛苦经历）配对，产生条件反应（恐惧/回避纽扣）。

AS I&D

Issues & Debates（AS 范围 5 个）

⚠️ 注意：AS syllabus（p.15）只列 5 个 issues，不是 6 个。Reductionism/Holism、Free Will/Determinism 是 A2 (Paper 4) 议题，不进 AS 答卷。

1️⃣ Application of psychology to everyday life / 心理学在日常生活中的应用

把核心研究结论迁移到现实场景（如 Milgram → obedience to authority 在 workplace / Milgram variations Burger 2009；Baron-Cohen → autism screening；Hoelzel → corporate mindfulness）。AO2 常考："Suggest two applications of [study] to everyday life."

把核心研究应用到现实场景是 CIE AS 高频题型。

2️⃣ Individual & situational explanations / 个体与情境解释

Individual = 把行为归因为个人特质/内部原因（如 Hassett 解释 toy preference = genetic/biological）；Situational = 归因为外部环境/情境（如 same Hassett finding could be explained by environmental socialisation）。辩论焦点：行为主要由哪方决定？Hassett 等跨物种研究支持 individual（biological）侧，Milgram 变异支持 situational 侧。

AS 焦点：到底是"性格决定命运"还是"情境决定行为"？

3️⃣ Nature vs nurture / 先天 vs 后天

Nature = 先天生物/基因；Nurture = 后天环境/经验。支持 nature 的研究：Hassett (跨物种+动物研究) · Dement (脑电+睡眠生理) · Hoelzel (脑结构改变) · Baron-Cohen (性别差异+先天 ToM 缺陷)。支持 nurture 的研究：Saavedra (纽扣恐惧) · Bandura (观察学习) · Fagen (训练) · Andrade (涂鸦提升记忆)。

答题框架：[Study X] supports the [nature/nurture] side because [specific finding shows ...]，且 MS 接受两种 valid 对比（如 Hassett = nature side / Saavedra = nurture side）。

CIE AS 必考 debate 之一，AO3 题型常见。

4️⃣ Use of children in psychological research / 使用儿童作为研究对象

涉及研究：Baron-Cohen（成人+儿童版本 ToM）· Bandura (1961 children) · Pozzulo (110 个年龄 6/8/10/12 儿童) · Perry (young adults + children eye-contact)。
伦理争议：informed consent (children can't give) · protection from harm (Bandura potentially distressing Bobo) · right to withdraw (often waived) · BPS guidelines on children。
辩护：研究为了解认知/社会发展必不可少；研究后做了 debrief；学校 + 家长同意 + 教师在旁监督。

CIE 必考：孩子在 4 篇 Study 出现，注意"伦理可辩护 vs 不可辩护"区别。

5️⃣ Use of animals in psychological research / 使用动物作为研究对象

涉及研究：Fagen (elephants) · Hassett (rhesus monkeys)。Dement 用的是人类（不是动物）。
伦理争议：动物 welfare 是否被损害 · 物种差异能否 generalize 到人类 · 道德地位。
辩护：Fagen 用 positive reinforcement（无电击）· Hassett 仅观察自然 toy preference 无 invasive procedure · 研究为了解 elephant cognition / 进化心理学有不可替代价值。

极少见动物议题；Fagen/Hassett 是首选例子。Dement 不是动物实验。

  1. "One debate is [debate name]." (1 mark AO1 = naming the debate)
  2. "[Study X] can be related to this debate because [specific finding] supports the [side]." (AO2/AO3 = application)
  3. Counter："However, this is limited because [counterargument]." (AO3 = evaluation, only for 4-mark I&D questions)

🎯 Paper 1 Section A · I&D / Application 类型样题

Paper 1 Section A 不直接考 assumption，但 Application / I&D 题需熟读 assumption 文本来答。

Paper 1 / Q5 style · 4 marks · AO2
"Suggest two applications of the findings of the study by Milgram (1963) to everyday life."
Application 1: Findings can be applied to workplace safety training — knowing that the actual presence of an authority figure influences obedience, employers can design whistleblower systems where employees report safety violations without their manager being present in the room.

Application 2: Findings can be applied to modern remote work settings — if actual presence reduces obedience, organisations should require critical decisions (disciplinary action, financial) to be made in writing/video, with an appeals process that does not involve the direct manager in real-time.
Paper 1 / Q7 style · 2 marks · AO1
"Outline what is meant by the individual/situational explanations debate."
Individual explanations attribute behaviour to personal characteristics (genes, personality, biology), while situational explanations attribute behaviour to the external environment or social context (e.g., Milgram's obedience was driven by the situation, not participants' personalities). The debate concerns whether behaviour is mainly due to the person or the environment.
Paper 1 / Q7 style · 4 marks · AO1+AO3
"Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) can be related to the nature-nurture debate. Discuss."
Nature side (AO2): Baron-Cohen supports nature because males scored lower than females on the Eyes Test, suggesting sex differences in theory of mind may have a biological basis (possibly prenatal testosterone).

Counter — Nurture side (AO3): However, this can be challenged because socialisation differences could also explain why females perform better (e.g., girls are encouraged to read facial expressions more). The study cannot definitively separate nature from nurture.
