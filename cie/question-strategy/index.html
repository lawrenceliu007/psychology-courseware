题型一 · Background 历史线 Q8 · 5 分 · 1 mark per point

怎么答

问的是研究之前的事：为什么要做这个研究。

  1. 写现实问题（1 句）：这个研究想解决什么现实问题。
  2. 写前人研究（2–3 句）：每句 = 人名 + 年份 + 一句发现。这里最容易拿分。
  3. 写研究缺口（1 句）：所以还需要进一步研究。
  • 研究本身的做法和结果，写了不给分。
  • 只写人名不写发现，不给分。
  • 定义最多写 1 句——题目给的两个定义只能算 1 分。

12 个研究

Piliavin et al. (1969) 已考 · m26_12

  1. Previous studies had shown that bystanders might derogate the characters of victims, and that people who are partly responsible for their plight would receive less sympathy/helping.
  2. People may show diffusion of responsibility — believing others in a group will help instead.
  3. Triggered by a real-life crime: Kitty Genovese was murdered in New York (1964); news reports alleged 38 people witnessed/heard it and no one intervened. （案例最多 2 分）
  4. Darley and Latané reported that bystanders hearing someone have an epileptic fit were less likely to help if they thought others were around.
  5. Latane and Rodin reported people were less likely to help someone who had fallen if group members were strangers.
  6. Bryan and Test reported people were more likely to help if they had just witnessed someone else helping.
  7. Much of the work on bystander intervention had been conducted in the laboratory, and previous research tended to test emergencies that were only heard, not seen.

雷区：cost-reward matrix 不给分；写 Piliavin 自己的地铁实验不算分。定义可换分：diffusion of responsibility / bystander apathy。

Pozzulo et al. (2011) 已考 · s26_11（另 3 分版 w25_11）

  1. Researchers have been interested in whether children are reliable (eye)witnesses — sometimes children are the only witness to a crime.
  2. Mistaken eyewitness identification has led to wrongful convictions.
  3. Children's eyewitness recall is known to be less reliable than an adult's.
  4. Children and adults can identify from a target-present line-up with accuracy; however, children are more likely to pick out an innocent person from a target-absent line-up.
  5. Both cognitive factors (matching a previous memory with a person in the line-up — more likely in target-present) and social factors (presence/pressure of an authority figure, fear of getting into trouble — more likely in target-absent) could affect identifications.
  6. Children are more likely to produce a false-positive response (choosing someone who is not the culprit while believing they are right).
  7. A previous study (Pozzulo & Lindsay, 1997) showed children are less likely to say "I don't know" than adults.

雷区：false memories 整个话题不给分。定义可换分：EWT 或 false-positive（二选一）。3 分版只写 social factors 一条线（authority figure → child intimidated → chooses to avoid trouble / please the officer → line-up presentation implies a choice must be made）。

Andrade (2010) 已考 · s26_12

  1. It had been assumed that doing concurrent tasks (e.g. doodling) impairs concentration / distracts from the primary task.
  2. Daydreaming had been linked to moments of boredom, and boredom plays a role in paying attention to information — its effects on processing had been overlooked.
  3. Arousal levels need to be maintained to be able to concentrate.
  4. Information processing can be difficult when asked to do "two things at once" (dual/multi-task designs investigate our cognitive resources).
  5. However, performance decrements may be lessened if a secondary task reduces mind-wandering / increases arousal.
  6. A secondary task can aid concentration if it does not compete with the primary task for cognitive resources (e.g. one visual and one audio task).
  7. Doodling could therefore increase concentration/arousal — whether doodling aids concentration/memory had never been experimentally tested.

雷区：central executive、daydreaming 的作用机制不给分。定义可换分：daydreaming / multi-tasking / doodling 三选一。

Hölzel et al. (2011) 已考 · s26_13

  1. Mindfulness has been shown to have positive psychological/mental effects and has been increasingly used in therapy to help patients.
  2. It has been shown to help reduce anxiety/depression/pain.
  3. Neuroimaging has been used to explore the neural mechanisms linked to mindfulness — including EEGs and fMRIs.
  4. Previous research showed anatomical differences in grey matter/brain structure in people who had engaged in meditation for a long time (cross-sectional).
  5. The hippocampus is an area of interest as it is one area that "changes" during meditation/mindfulness practice.
  6. Research has been clearly showing that neural systems can be modified in people engaging in mindfulness-type activities.
  7. There had been no/limited studies looking at the effect longitudinally — the gap this study filled.

雷区：stress / hormones 与 mindfulness 的关联不给分。1 分可给研究 aim。

Perry et al. (2015) 已考 3 分版 · w25_12（5 分版未考）

  1. 3 分 biological 版（已考官方点）：Oxytocin plays a role in personal space/empathy/social bonding.
  2. Lesions in the amygdala appear to reduce the need for personal space; amygdala activity in healthy individuals is correlated with feeling uncomfortable at close distances.
  3. Amygdala activation is affected by levels of oxytocin; oxytocin can also affect the personal space needed between self and someone perceived as attractive.
  4. 5 分背景补充：Edward T. Hall (1966) proposed four interpersonal distance zones (intimate <46cm / personal 46–122cm / social 122–210cm / public) based on middle-class American preferences — a "bubble" that moves with the person.
  5. Beaulieu (2004) showed cultural factors affect personal space: in no-contact cultures people touch less and stand further apart.
  6. Oxytocin is a social hormone regulating social thinking/behaviour — but its effects are not universally positive (also linked to jealousy and lack of cooperation with out-groups).

3 分版 Do credit "healthy amygdala may prefer further interpersonal distance"；不给分：Perry 的 procedure/findings。

Saavedra & Silverman (2002) 已考 3 分版 · w25_13（5 分版未考）

  1. 3 分 disgust 版（已考官方点）：Disgust is a feeling of aversion towards something offensive/something we fear; disgust interacting with fear can result in avoidance behaviour(s).
  2. Children can negatively evaluate a specific phobic object without any anticipation of threat — so it must be disgust, not fear, causing the negative evaluation.
  3. A study measured disgust in children with a spider phobia and it decreased as fear decreased (De Jong et al., 1997) — a link between disgust and fear.
  4. 5 分背景补充：Phobia is an irrational, persistent fear of an object/event out of proportion to actual danger.
  5. Classical conditioning was first studied by Pavlov (1927); Watson & Rayner (1920) ("Little Albert") showed phobias can be learned by associating a neutral stimulus with a frightening one.
  6. Hepburn & Page (1999) suggested treating disgust (not just fear) helped adult blood-phobia patients; disgust was called "the forgotten emotion of psychiatry" (Phillips et al., 1998).
  7. Disgust-based negative evaluation in childhood phobias had never been targeted in treatment — the gap.

雷区：disgust 版不可用研究中男孩的例子。Do credit "usually triggered by a traumatic event in childhood"。

Fagen et al. (2014) psychology 版已考 · s24_12（5 分版未考）

  1. Operant conditioning is learning by consequence: if you are rewarded you are more likely to repeat a behaviour; if punished, less likely.
  2. A primary reinforcer fulfils a biological need (e.g. food).
  3. A secondary reinforcer can be exchanged for a primary reinforcer; in secondary positive reinforcement (SPR) training a sound (e.g. whistle) is consistently followed by the primary reinforcer.
  4. 5 分背景补充：Trunk wash is needed to screen captive elephants for tuberculosis, which infects 11–25% of captive elephants and is transmissible to humans (zoonotic transfer).
  5. Traditional elephant training relied on punishment and negative reinforcement (fear/pain), harming animal welfare.
  6. SPR had been successfully applied to pandas, primates and antelopes, but had never been systematically studied and quantitatively documented in elephants — the gap.

雷区（psychology 版）：ignore classical conditioning。There are other creditworthy responses, including shaping and behavioural chaining。

Hassett et al. (2008) psychology 版已考 · s25_13（5 分版未考）

  1. Sex differences refer to behavioural differences based on biological differences between males and females.
  2. These can be based on hormones/chromosomes: the main male hormone is testosterone; the main female hormone is oestrogen.
  3. Play is any mental/physical activity performed in leisure time for enjoyment; socialisation is any mechanism that changes/adapts behaviour to societal/cultural expectations.
  4. 5 分背景补充：Children show gendered toy play (boys: active/aggressive toys; girls: nurturing toys), long assumed to be the result of socialisation and gender stereotypes.
  5. In children it is impossible to separate biology from socialisation; rare studies of girls exposed to pre-natal androgens showed stronger preference for "boys' toys" despite feminine socialisation.
  6. Alexander and Hines (2002) found a similar pattern in vervet monkeys — monkeys can provide a model in which socialisation is removed, testing whether toy preferences are biologically driven.

雷区（psychology 版）：do not accept any answer about gender of monkeys；do credit nature–nurture 描述 1 分。

Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) psychology 版已考 · w25_13（5 分版未考）

  1. Theory of Mind refers to our ability to attribute mental states to ourselves and others — understanding that others may have different ideas and hold different emotions to us.
  2. It is linked to empathy: the ability to understand how other people are feeling/thinking, and to use this knowledge to explain and predict others' actions.
  3. ASD affects about 1% of the population; ASD/AS/HFA individuals have difficulties in social functioning and communication.
  4. An earlier version of the Eyes Test (Baron-Cohen, 1997) found adults with AS/HFA correctly identified fewer emotions from the eyes than controls.
  5. The old test had only 2 options (often semantic opposites) and 25 sets of eyes, producing a ceiling effect; some items could be answered from gaze direction alone; some emotion words were too difficult.
  6. Existing ToM measures were mostly designed for children — an adult-appropriate test with greater sensitivity was needed (the revised test: 4 options, 36 sets, glossary, judged by 8 panels).

Do credit descriptions of AS/HFA for 1 mark max；研究例子（Eyes Test 本身）可换 1 分。

Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961) ★预测

  1. Children copy adults — the immediate social setting encourages a child to imitate what he/she is watching.
  2. Social learning is the learning of a new behaviour observed in a role model and imitated later in the absence of that model; it requires the observer to pay attention, retain and reproduce the behaviour.
  3. Previous research had only shown that children imitate a model in the presence of the model — whether the behaviour would be reproduced without the model present was unknown.
  4. Bandura had previously conducted experiments on social learning and wanted to test whether social learning theory could explain aggression specifically.
  5. In the USA in the 1960s, boys were rewarded for sex-appropriate behaviours and punished for inappropriate ones; aggression was seen as a masculine-type behaviour.
  6. It was therefore expected that children would imitate same-sex models more, and that boys would show more readiness to imitate aggression.

定义可换分：social learning / model / aggression。第 3 点"前人局限"是本研究的招牌背景点。

Milgram (1963) ★预测

  1. During World War II / the Holocaust, millions of innocent people were systematically murdered on command — this required the obedience of many ordinary citizens, not just Nazi officers.
  2. Obedience is following the orders of an authority figure; destructive obedience is obedience causing potential psychological or physical harm to another person.
  3. One explanation was the "Germans are different" hypothesis — that German citizens possessed defective personal traits making extreme obedience possible (an individual explanation).
  4. Milgram sought to challenge this by testing a situational explanation: whether ordinary people of any nationality would obey harmful orders.
  5. Before the study, Milgram described the procedure to psychology students and colleagues: they predicted only an insignificant minority would go to the end — estimates from 0% to 3% (class mean 1.2%).
  6. The core question was why ordinary people would obey an authority's commands even when these harm another person.

注意：Hofling et al. (1966) 在 Milgram 之后，不能写进 Milgram 的背景。"0–3% 预测"发生在研究之前，可给分。

Dement & Kleitman (1957) ★预测

  1. Sleep is an altered state of consciousness; dreams are vivid, generally visual sequences of imagery during sleep.
  2. Traditionally, dreaming was studied only through dream recall — a subjective method limited by forgetting and impossible to verify while the dream was happening.
  3. When participants in earlier research were randomly woken, they sometimes reported no dream, for reasons that could not be explained — an objective method was needed.
  4. Aserinsky and Kleitman (1955) first applied objective physiological measurement (EEG) to sleep: participants woken from REM sleep were more likely to report vivid visual dreams than those woken at other times.
  5. They found rapid eye movements occur in both eyes simultaneously, in cycles of roughly 90 minutes; during REM other body muscles are inactive and breathing/heart rate increase.
  6. EEG can detect that dreaming is occurring but cannot read dream content — so waking participants to report dreams was combined with EOG eye-movement recording.

定义可换分：REM / nREM / dream。Aserinsky & Kleitman (1955) 是必写前人研究（Kleitman 本人是原作者之一）。

题型二 · Psychology 概念链 3–4 分 · "Describe the psychology being investigated"

怎么答

问的是研究背后的概念——像写一段课本定义，不是写研究做了什么。

  1. 总概念写一句话定义。
  2. 拆 2–3 个子概念，各写一句定义——这是主要得分点。
  3. 最后可以加 1 句本研究的例子，最多换 1 分。
  • 只写术语名不展开（如光写 "Theory of Mind"），不给分。
  • Fagen：别写 classical conditioning；Hassett：别写猴子的性别，要写人类层面的内容。

另一种问法（2 分）：approach 版 "Explain why this study is from the X approach"

怎么答

问的是这个研究为什么属于某个流派。两句话拿 2 分。

  1. 写该流派的一句假设（用下表原句）。
  2. 接一句 "This was clearly shown here as…" + 本研究的做法或结果。
  • 只描述研究、不解释"为什么属于这个流派" = 0 分。
流派假设（背原句）
SocialBehaviour, cognitions and emotions are influenced by social contexts, social environments and groups / by the actual, implied or imagined presence of others.
CognitiveInput–process–output：信息输入 → 加工 → 输出（计算机类比）。
Learning行为由经验习得：classical / operant conditioning（强化与惩罚）、social learning（观察与模仿）。
Biological行为有生物基础：hormones、brain structures（amygdala / hippocampus / grey matter）、genetics。

Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961) ★预测

  1. Social learning is the learning of a new behaviour observed in a role model and imitated later in the absence of that model.
  2. A model is a person whose behaviour is observed and may be imitated — imitation is copying the behaviour of the model.
  3. Social learning requires the observer to pay attention to the model's behaviour, retain it in memory, and be able to reproduce it.
  4. Aggression is behaviour intended to harm another person (physically or verbally); the study tested whether social learning theory could explain aggression specifically.
  5. Imitation is more likely when the model is similar to the observer (e.g. same sex) and when the modelled behaviour is rewarded rather than punished.
  6. The children watched an adult model behave aggressively towards a Bobo doll and were then observed alone with the doll (example mark).

概念族：social learning / model / imitation / attention–retention–reproduction / aggression / same-sex similarity。别把"1960s 美国性别社会化"当主力写——那是 background 版的得分点。

Milgram (1963) ★预测

  1. Obedience is following the orders/instructions of an authority figure.
  2. Destructive obedience is obedience that causes potential psychological or physical harm to another person.
  3. Authority figures can command obedience because of their perceived position/status (uniform, title, institutional setting) rather than personal power.
  4. An individual (dispositional) explanation attributes obedience to the person's own traits — e.g. the "Germans are different" hypothesis claimed German citizens had defective traits making extreme obedience possible.
  5. A situational explanation attributes obedience to the social situation — whether ordinary people of any nationality would obey harmful orders.
  6. Participants were ordered by an experimenter in a lab coat to give electric shocks up to 450V to a "learner" (example mark).

概念族：obedience / destructive obedience / authority / dispositional vs situational explanation。"0–3% 预测"、Holocaust 动因是 background 版得分点，此处最多作引入一句。

Piliavin et al. (1969) ★预测（approach 版已考 m24_12 · 2 分）

  1. Bystander intervention is about whether a witness to an emergency helps — and how the social situation affects that decision.
  2. Diffusion of responsibility is believing that others in a group will help instead, so each individual feels less personally responsible.
  3. Bystander apathy is the phenomenon of witnesses in a group failing to intervene while someone needs help.
  4. Helping may depend on characteristics of the victim — people who seem partly responsible for their own plight, or who are drunk/undesirable, receive less sympathy and less help.
  5. Witnessing someone else helping can model helping behaviour and increase it in observers.
  6. On a subway train, a person collapsed and either a model or no one helped, and the speed of helping by other passengers was timed (example mark).

概念族：bystander intervention / diffusion of responsibility / bystander apathy / victim characteristics / modelling of helping。approach 版满分公式：social approach 假设一句 + "This was clearly shown here as…"（model 条件 / group size）挂钩一句。

Perry et al. (2015) ★预测（3 分 outline 版已考 w25_12）

  1. Personal space is the invisible "bubble" around a person; intruding into it causes discomfort — it moves with the person wherever they go.
  2. Oxytocin is a social hormone that regulates social thinking and behaviour, playing a role in empathy and social bonding.
  3. The amygdala is a brain structure involved in detecting threat and social distance: lesions appear to reduce the need for personal space, and amygdala activity in healthy individuals correlates with feeling uncomfortable at close distances.
  4. Amygdala activation is affected by levels of oxytocin — oxytocin can change the personal space kept from others.
  5. Biological approach assumption: behaviour and emotions are explained by biological structures (brain areas) and hormones, rather than by conscious choice.
  6. Participants received intranasal oxytocin or placebo and judged the comfort of interpersonal distances shown in images (example mark).

概念族：personal space / oxytocin / amygdala / biological approach 假设。已考的 3 分 outline 版（biological factors）只需中间三条生物线。

Dement & Kleitman (1957) ★预测

  1. Sleep is an altered state of consciousness; a dream is a vivid, generally visual sequence of imagery experienced during sleep.
  2. REM (rapid eye movement) sleep is a sleep stage in which the eyes move rapidly and simultaneously; during REM other body muscles are inactive while breathing and heart rate increase.
  3. REM alternates with non-REM (nREM) sleep in cycles of roughly 90 minutes through the night.
  4. Dream recall is a subjective method of studying dreaming — limited by forgetting and impossible to verify while the dream is happening.
  5. An objective, physiological measure (EEG for brain activity, EOG for eye movements) can detect that dreaming is occurring but cannot read dream content.
  6. Participants slept in a lab, were woken at various points (including during REM and nREM) and asked to report whether they had been dreaming (example mark).

概念族：altered state of consciousness / dream / REM–nREM 与 90 分钟周期 / dream recall 的主观性 / 生理测量（EEG/EOG）的能与不能。⚠️ psychology 版里 Aserinsky & Kleitman (1955) 不是主体——那是 background 版的必写点。

Saavedra & Silverman (2002) ★预测（3 分 outline 版已考 w25_13）

  1. A specific phobia is an irrational, persistent fear of a particular object or event that is out of proportion to any actual danger.
  2. Phobias are usually triggered by a traumatic event in childhood, and can be learned: Watson and Rayner (1920) showed a neutral stimulus (white rat) paired with a frightening event (loud noise) created "Little Albert's" fear — classical conditioning of fear.
  3. Disgust is a feeling of aversion towards something offensive; disgust interacting with fear can result in avoidance behaviour.
  4. Children can negatively evaluate a phobic object without any anticipation of threat — meaning it is disgust, not fear, causing the negative evaluation.
  5. Systematic desensitisation is a behavioural therapy that treats phobias by gradually exposing the person to the feared object while teaching relaxation — the fear hierarchy pairs feared stimuli with an incompatible (relaxed) response.
  6. A boy with a phobia of buttons was treated with systematic desensitisation using a fear hierarchy and Feelings Thermometer ratings (example mark).

概念族：specific phobia / classical conditioning（Watson & Rayner）/ disgust vs fear / systematic desensitisation + fear hierarchy。与 Fagen 雷区相反：S&S 的 psychology 就是 conditioning——这里是合法得分点。

Hölzel et al. (2011) ★预测（background 版已考 s26_13）

  1. Mindfulness is intentionally paying attention to the present moment in a non-judgemental way.
  2. MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction) is a structured 8-week programme of mindfulness training, used to reduce stress and increasingly used in therapy.
  3. Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to change its structure (not just its activity) in response to experience and practice.
  4. Grey matter is brain tissue containing the cell bodies of neurons; the hippocampus is a structure important for memory and learning, and an area of interest because it "changes" during mindfulness practice.
  5. Measuring brain structure requires neuroimaging — MRI scans can show anatomical differences in grey matter between people.
  6. Participants completed an 8-week MBSR course and their brains were scanned before and after, compared with a wait-list control group (example mark).

Milgram (1963) 已考 · reliability m26_12 · sampling s25_11

S1 · Reliability / Standardisation： The study had a highly standardised procedure, making it very reliable. The four prods were always delivered in the same fixed order ("Please continue… The experiment requires that you continue…"), and the learner's protests were scripted, from the grunt at 75V to the scream and pounding on the wall at 270V, followed by complete silence. Even the learner's answer pattern was fixed at roughly three wrong to one correct. Therefore, anyone could replicate the exact procedure and test whether comparable obedience levels are obtained.

S2 · Validity (experimental realism)： The study had high internal/experimental realism, so it measured genuine obedience rather than play-acting. Every participant received a real 45V sample shock from the generator before starting, and the machine looked authentic with 30 labelled switches from 15V to 450V, buzzes and flashing lights. In fact, no participant suspected the shocks were simulated, and participants afterwards rated the learner's pain at a mean of 13.42 out of 14. Therefore, the 65% full-obedience rate reflects what people truly believed they were doing to another person.

W1 · Ethics： Participants were deceived (the shocks were fake, the draw was rigged so the naive participant always became the teacher) and were exposed to psychological harm: observers noted profuse sweating, trembling and stuttering, 14 of the 40 participants showed nervous laughter, and 3 suffered uncontrollable seizures — one so severe that the session had to be stopped early. Although a friendly debrief followed, by modern standards the distress should have been terminated far earlier. Therefore, the benefit of the findings has to be weighed against the cost to the participants.

W2 · Sampling technique / Generalisability： All 40 participants were males aged 20–50 recruited through a newspaper advertisement offering $4.50, i.e. a volunteer (self-selected) sample from one small US area around New Haven. Volunteers attracted by such an ad may be more cooperative or more deferential to authority than the general population, and women and other cultures are entirely excluded. Therefore, the 65% obedience rate may not generalise beyond obediently-inclined American males of the 1960s.

已考过的指定议题：reliability（m26_12）· sampling technique（s25_11）。可以写：ethics（W1）；quantitative data（最大电压 65% 到 450V、全数至少 300V、均值 368V）；ecological validity / generalisations（W2 同素材）。

Piliavin et al. (1969) 已考 · ethics w24_13 · quantitative s26_12

S1 · Ecological validity： The study has high ecological validity because it took place in a real setting with real participants. Over 103 trials, around 4,450 genuine New York subway passengers witnessed the staged collapse on the A/D express line between Harlem and the Bronx, and none of them knew an experiment was taking place. Therefore, the helping behaviour observed was completely natural and free of demand characteristics, unlike behaviour produced in a laboratory.

S2 · Quantitative data： The study collected quantitative data, which makes meaningful comparisons between conditions possible. Observers timed how quickly the first helper responded (in seconds) and counted how many people helped and of which race and gender. As a result, the 95% helping rate for the cane victim could be directly compared with the 50% rate for the drunk victim, showing that the type of victim strongly affects helping. This also produced an unexpected trend — larger groups tended to help faster (median 1.5s with 7+ males in the critical area), contrary to what diffusion of responsibility predicts.

W1 · Ethics： None of the ~4,450 passengers gave informed consent or was debriefed — they never learned the collapse was staged. They were deceived into believing a drunk or ill victim genuinely needed help, and passengers who failed to help may have experienced guilt or anxiety afterwards. Privacy was also invaded, as female observers secretly noted down passengers' remarks. Therefore, the researchers prioritised scientific value over participants' rights to a degree that would not be permitted today.

W2 · Validity (internal — limited control)： As a field experiment, the study had limited control over extraneous variables, reducing internal validity. The researchers could not standardise who happened to be in the carriage: group size, the mix of passengers and even time of day varied across the 103 trials. Worse, the researchers' own records show that Team 2 ran the wrong condition on Day 4 because the victim "didn't like" playing drunk, and there was only one Black victim, so any race-of-victim comparison rests on very limited data. Therefore, some differences between conditions may be confounded rather than caused by the victim type alone.

已考过的指定议题：ethics（w24_13）· quantitative data（s26_12）。可以写：ecological validity 在此研究是 strength（别写反）；validity（W2）；reliability（同一 victim 装束、70 秒固定倒下、同一路线）。

Perry et al. (2015) ★预测

S1 · Reliability / Controls (double-blind)： The study used rigorous controls that make the findings reliable and free from researcher bias. Administration of 24 IU intranasal oxytocin versus a saline placebo was double-blind, so neither the participants nor the experimenters knew who received the hormone, and the two test sessions were counterbalanced one week apart. All stimuli were computer-generated and presented in exactly the same way to every participant (each room pair displayed for 2 seconds). Therefore, the differences in preferred distance cannot be explained by researcher expectations or order effects, and the procedure could be replicated exactly.

S2 · Ethics： The study met ethical guidelines to a high standard, which is a strength compared with classic social studies such as Milgram or Piliavin. It was approved by the ethics committees of both the University of Haifa and Hadassah Medical Centre (appropriate for a drug study), all participants gave informed consent, and they were fully debriefed about the real aims. The only deception was the cover story in Experiment 2 (that they would later discuss personal topics with another participant), which the researchers judged unlikely to cause distress. Therefore, the study shows obedience and personal-space research can be done ethically without losing scientific value.

W1 · Ecological validity： Participants judged how close a computer-drawn figure should approach on a screen by pressing the spacebar, or chose between computer-generated colour images of rooms with chairs at fixed distances (20–140 cm) and angles. Real personal-space behaviour depends on dynamic social cues such as facial expression, tone of voice and gestures, none of which were present. Therefore, the distances preferred on a computer task may not reflect how people would actually position themselves in a real face-to-face encounter.

W2 · Generalisability： All 54 participants (40 in the final high/low empathy groups) were male undergraduates aged 19–32 from a single Israeli university, participating for course credit or payment. Personal-space norms are known to vary with gender and culture (e.g. no-contact cultures differ from contact cultures), and females were excluded entirely, even though oxytocin is a social hormone involved in social behaviour. Therefore, the finding that oxytocin narrows distance for high-empathy but widens it for low-empathy men may not apply to women or to other cultures.

已考过的指定议题：未考（10 分题未出，很可能考）。可以写：ecological validity（W1 最可能）· ethics（S2 作 strength）· reliability（S1）· quantitative data（Exp1 电脑任务四类人物的平均距离占比：Stranger 39.82% > Authority 34.12% > Ball 20.20% > Friend 12.46%）· self-reports（IRI 分组的社会赞许偏差）。

Andrade (2010) 已考 · sampling w24_12 · eco validity s26_11

S1 · Reliability / Standardisation： The procedure was highly standardised, giving the study good reliability. Every participant heard exactly the same mock telephone message from a cassette recording, delivered in a fairly monotone voice at 227 words per minute, in a quiet, visually dull room. The doodling task was also standardised — an A4 sheet of alternating squares and circles to shade with a pencil — and the order of the two recall tests was counterbalanced across participants. Therefore, another researcher could replicate the procedure exactly and check whether the 29% memory advantage for doodlers is reproducible.

S2 · Validity (controls)： Participants were randomly assigned to the doodling or control condition, and in the doodling group the experimenter actively encouraged shading so that the IV (doing a concurrent task) was genuinely in operation. Memory performance was carefully operationalised as the number of correct names/places minus false alarms, and plausible mishearings (e.g. "Greg" for "Craig") still counted as correct. Therefore, the group difference can confidently be attributed to doodling rather than to chance differences between the groups.

W1 · Sampling technique / Generalisability： All 40 participants came from the participant panel of the MRC Applied Psychology Unit — people who had volunteered to take part in research and were paid a small honorarium — and 35 of the 40 were female. Panel volunteers may be more cooperative or more practiced at laboratory tasks than the general population. Therefore, the 29% recall advantage may not generalise to people who have not volunteered for research or to male participants.

W2 · Ecological validity： Listening to a deliberately boring two-and-a-half-minute mock telephone message about a party while shading printed shapes is not an everyday activity, and the doodling itself was a standardised shading task rather than the free, self-directed doodling people actually do in real life (e.g. during a lecture or phone call). The study also never measured daydreaming, so it cannot be certain that doodling worked by preventing mind-wandering rather than in some other way. Therefore, we cannot be sure that real-life doodling would produce the same memory benefit.

已考过的指定议题：sampling technique（w24_12）· ecological validity（s26_11）。可以写：reliability（S1）；validity（S2 控制 / W2 生态两面）；quantitative data（monitoring 7.7 vs 6.9）；ethics（mild deception + surprise test）。

Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) 已考 · ethics s24_12 · quantitative s25_12

S1 · Validity (control tasks)： The design included clever controls that make the conclusion about theory of mind specifically valid. The AS/HFA group also completed a gender-recognition control task on the same eyes and all scored at least 33 out of 36, showing they could process the visual information in the photos. A fourth, IQ-matched comparison group (mean IQ 116 vs 115 for the clinical group) ruled out intelligence as an explanation, and Eye Test scores correlated with the AQ questionnaire (r = −0.53) but not with IQ. Therefore, the deficit shown is specific to social cognition, not to perception or general ability.

S2 · Reliability / Quantitative data： Each of the 36 items was checked by a panel of 8 independent judges, with at least 5 agreeing on the target word before the item was kept, and all participants could consult the same glossary of terms, which removes vocabulary ability as a confound. Scoring was simply the number of correct emotion identifications, so performance could be compared directly across the four groups (AS/HFA 21.9 vs student comparison 28.0 vs IQ-matched 30.9). Therefore, the group differences are based on objective, replicable measurement rather than clinical judgement.

W1 · Generalisability： The AS/HFA group contained only 15 participants, all of them male, recruited through advertisements in an Autistic Society magazine (self-selected), and every eye photograph was of a Caucasian face. Females with AS/HFA may perform differently, and emotion recognition is harder across ethnic groups. Therefore, the claim that people with AS/HFA have impaired theory of mind may only be safely made about adult Caucasian men with the condition.

W2 · Ecological validity： Participants judged mental states from static, black-and-white photographs of the eye region alone, whereas in real life we infer what others think from whole moving faces, voices, gestures and context. The authors themselves recommended that future studies use videos. Therefore, a person scoring poorly on the Eyes Test may still function better in real social interaction than the score suggests — the test measures a narrow slice of theory of mind.

已考过的指定议题：quantitative data（s25_12）· ethics（s24_12——只认 minimising harm、informed consent、confidentiality 三条）。可以写：generalisations（W1）· ecological validity（W2）· reliability（8-judge 程序）。称其为 laboratory experiment 本身不计分。

Pozzulo et al. (2011) 已考 · ecological validity w25_12

S1 · Reliability / Standardisation： For every participant the instructions for the line-up were the same ("The person you saw may or may not be there. If the correct person is NOT there, please point to the silhouetted box"), the four target videos (Dora, Diego, and two human targets) were fixed 6-second silent clips shown in counterbalanced order, and the foil photographs were selected for similarity by three independent raters, then cropped to show only face, neck and shoulders in black and white. Therefore, the identification rates of children versus adults (e.g. 0.99 vs 0.95 for cartoons) can be compared fairly, and the procedure is replicable.

S2 · Ethics (model of ethical research with children)： Informed consent was obtained twice — written parental consent plus the child's own verbal assent in age-appropriate language — and children were explicitly and repeatedly told they could change their mind at any time without getting into trouble. The researchers first built rapport through craft activities, used only neutral cartoon and everyday videos rather than crime material, monitored the children for fatigue and anxiety, and every child received crayons and a colouring book at the end. Therefore, the study shows scientifically valuable developmental research can be conducted without compromising young participants' rights.

W1 · Ecological validity： Children and adults identified targets from a laptop line-up after watching a calm 6-second video in which the face was visible for only 2–3 seconds. A real eyewitness sees a possibly threatening event under stress and high arousal, and later makes an identification in a police station with real consequences. None of that pressure was present here. Therefore, the finding that children make more false identifications from target-absent line-ups (for cartoon faces, correct rejection only 0.74 vs adult 0.94) may underestimate or distort what happens in genuine forensic settings.

W2 · Generalisability / Confounds： The 59 children came from three private schools in Eastern Ontario, the 53 adults were university psychology students from a research participation pool who may have been more motivated to participate, and all human targets and foils were Caucasian faces. Cross-ethnic identification is known to be poorer, so own-race bias is an uncontrolled confounding variable. There is also no adolescent group between age 7 and 17, so the developmental trend cannot be traced. Therefore, the child/adult differences may partly reflect sampling rather than pure developmental effects.

已考过的指定议题：ecological validity（w25_12）。可以写：ethics（S2 作 strength）· quantitative data（0.99 vs 0.95；0.74 vs 0.94）· generalisations（W2，私校儿童 + 被试池成人）· reliability（S1）。

Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961) 已考 · validity w24_11 · generalisations m25_12 · ethics w25_11

S1 · Reliability (double inter-rater)： The aggressive model followed a fixed script — a roughly 9-minute sequence of hitting the 5-foot Bobo doll with the mallet, sitting on it and kicking it, accompanied by set phrases like "Kick him! Sock him! Hit him down!" — and in the test phase the toys were always presented in the same order while behaviour was recorded through a one-way mirror in 5-second intervals (240 response units per child). Two independent observers agreed closely on their ratings (inter-rater reliability of about r = 0.9), and the pre-study aggression ratings also agreed at r = 0.89. Therefore, the measurement of imitative aggression is objective, and the standardised procedure can be replicated.

S2 · Validity (controls)： Before the experiment, the experimenter and nursery teacher rated all 72 children on four 5-point aggression scales, and children were then matched into triplets of similar aggression and randomly assigned to the aggressive, non-aggressive or control conditions. Therefore, Bandura could be confident that differences in imitative aggression between groups (e.g. boys with a male model averaging 25.8 acts versus around 2 in the control group) were caused by the type of model the child had seen, not by pre-existing aggressiveness.

W1 · Generalisability： All 72 children were aged 3–6 and came from one Stanford University nursery school, meaning most were children of academic families, and there were only six children per experimental condition. The research also took place in 1961 America. Therefore, the finding that children imitate an aggressive model may not generalise to older children, other social classes or other cultures and eras.

W2 · Ecological validity / Ethics： A Bobo doll is a toy designed to be hit, so hitting it after watching an adult do so is not the same as real aggression; novel acts such as throwing the doll in the air and kicking it rarely occur in everyday life, and the laboratory playroom observed through a one-way mirror is not a natural learning environment. In addition, children were deliberately frustrated before the test (attractive toys were taken away and said to be reserved for other children) and were taught aggressive behaviour without any debrief, which would not pass modern ethical review. Therefore, both the realism of the task and the treatment of the children weaken what the study tells us about real aggressive learning.

已考过的指定议题：validity（w24_11）· generalisations（m25_12）· ethics（w25_11，3 次之最——官方范例 "psychologically harmed by watching an adult beat up a Bobo doll"）。可以写：quantitative data（25.8 vs 2.0 两面可写）· reliability（S1）· ecological validity（W2 前半）。

Saavedra & Silverman (2002) 已考 · quantitative s24_13 · subjective s25_13

S1 · Validity (method triangulation + follow-up)： The case study used method triangulation and long-term follow-up, giving it high validity for understanding this individual's phobia. Information came from interviews with both the boy and his mother, direct observation of his behaviour in therapy, and self-report ratings; the origin of the fear (a bowl of buttons falling on him during a kindergarten art project at age 5) emerged from these interviews. Crucially, follow-ups at 6 and 12 months showed the gains lasted — he no longer met diagnostic criteria and was able to wear his school uniform with small buttons every day. Therefore, the conclusion that disgust-focused imagery exposure works is supported by converging evidence over time, not just a single session's improvement.

S2 · Quantitative data： At every session the boy rated his distress on a Feelings Thermometer from 0 to 8, separately for each of the 11 hierarchy items (from large denim buttons rated 2 up to small coloured buttons rated 8), and the number of buttons he could handle was counted (rising from 1 to 3, 6 and then 11). As a result, the effect of each intervention could be compared over time — for example his rating for "hundreds of buttons falling on my body" fell from 8 to 3 across imagery exposure. Therefore, the therapists could make valid judgements about which treatment was working.

W1 · Generalisability： With a single participant, generalisation is extremely limited. The study describes one 9-year-old Hispanic American boy with a specific phobia of buttons — a very rare condition (koumpounophobia) that he had had for four years. Females may respond differently to this type of therapy, and people with more common phobias of concrete objects may not find a Feelings Thermometer useful if their feared object is not concrete. Therefore, the success of disgust-focused imagery exposure here cannot be assumed for other children, other phobias or other cultures.

W2 · Subjective data / Self-report bias： All distress ratings came from the boy's own Feelings Thermometer judgements, and the fear hierarchy was built from his own descriptions, so demand characteristics could have distorted the data — he was fully aware he was undergoing therapy and could have rated buttons as less frightening than he truly felt so as to appear to be improving, and his mother's own ratings could have been influenced by her desire to see her son improve. There was also no control condition, so the improvement cannot be compared against no-treatment or alternative treatment conditions. Therefore, the apparent effectiveness of the therapy partly rests on a single child's self-report.

已考过的指定议题：quantitative data（s24_13）· subjective data（s25_13）。可以写：self-reports（W2 同素材）· generalisations（W1）· ethics（consent 男孩+母亲、vulnerable participant、刻意暴露的暂时 distress）· validity（S1）。

Fagen et al. (2014) 已考 · quantitative s24_11

S1 · Quantitative data： For every task the researchers counted the number of sessions, the mean session length, the number of offers required and the percentage of correct responses at each test, with a strict pass criterion of 80% correct over 10 trials. As a result, learning could be tracked numerically — the group's average success rose from 36.0% at session 10 to 89.3% by session 35, and the fastest elephant (Elephant 2) completed training in 25 sessions totalling 257 minutes while the adult needed 424 minutes and still failed. Therefore, the effectiveness of secondary-positive-reinforcement training could be objectively demonstrated rather than just described.

S2 · Ethics (animal welfare)： The training method was ethically strong for animal research. It used only secondary positive reinforcement — a whistle as a conditioned marker followed by chopped banana — with no punishment or coercion at all, and participation was voluntary: an elephant could simply turn away. The research also had clear welfare value, since trunk washes are needed to screen captive elephants for tuberculosis, which infects 11–25% of captive elephants and is transmissible to humans. Therefore, the study follows the principles of minimising harm and using reward rather than deprivation in a way that benefits both the animals and their keepers.

W1 · Generalisability： Only five female elephants from a single working stable in Nepal were studied — four juveniles aged 5–7 and one adult of about 50 — and the adult failed the training despite 424 minutes of work. Therefore, the finding that elephants can learn a trunk wash through reinforcement in about a week may only apply to young captive females in this setting, and not to adult elephants, males, or elephants in different housing conditions.

W2 · Reliability / Objectivity： Whether an elephant's behaviour was good enough for a real trunk wash was decided by the trainer — the first author of the study — with no third-party assessment and no video review, and the training plan was adapted individually for each elephant rather than following a fixed protocol. Therefore, another trainer might have judged the same performances differently, which weakens the objectivity and replicability of the findings.

已考过的指定议题：quantitative data（s24_11）。可以写：ethics（S2，SPR 无惩罚）· generalisations（W1）· reliability（W2 是弱点方向）· validity（真实 working stable 的 ecological validity 作 strength）。雷区：写"用大象所以 demand characteristics 低"不给分；称 field experiment 不给分。

Hassett et al. (2008) 已考 · ethics m24_12 · generalisations s26_13

S1 · Reliability (operationalisation + double observers)： The observation was systematic and objective. The researchers defined a checklist of 15 specific behaviours (extended touch, hold, sit on, carry, drag, sniff, mouth, throw and so on) and recorded both the frequency and the exact duration of each from two video cameras, with two observers working together to reach a consensus on coding. Each trial was a standard 25 minutes, the plush and wheeled toys were always placed 10 metres apart, and their left–right positions were counterbalanced. Therefore, the measurements of toy interaction are replicable and not dependent on a single observer's impression.

S2 · Ethics (animal guidelines)： The study followed animal-research ethical guidelines closely. It was approved by Emory University's animal care committee; the monkeys remained in their established natal groups in a 25 × 25 metre outdoor enclosure with temperature-controlled indoor access, were fed monkey chow twice daily with fruit and vegetables and constant water, and were observed only by video cameras, which would not have been distressing — the only incident was one plush toy being torn apart. Therefore, in terms of minimising harm, appropriate housing and reward-free non-invasive procedures, the study satisfies the standards expected of primate research.

W1 · Generalisability： Although 135 monkeys lived in the troop, analyses were based on only 34 individuals (11 males, 23 females) from one research station, all living in captivity in their natal groups — some troop members were excluded because they were too young to identify reliably, and monkeys with fewer than five recorded behaviours on the checklist were dropped. Therefore, the sex-typed toy preferences found may only apply to this captive troop and not to wild monkeys, where no human toys are available at all.

W2 · Validity (toy categorisation and comparison)： The toys were chosen for their object properties (wheeled versus plush) rather than being validated as "masculine" or "feminine", so the preference difference might actually be driven by other features such as texture or how the toy moves. The famous comparison with children's data (Berenbaum & Hines, 1992) is also not like-for-like: the children were tested individually and only duration was measured, while the monkeys were tested as a group and frequency was the key measure — and presentation style is known to affect toy preference results. Therefore, the claim that monkey play parallels human gendered play is built on non-parallel measurements.

Hölzel et al. (2011) 已考 · self-reports w25_13

S1 · Validity (objective physiological measure + control group)： Grey-matter concentration was assessed by structural MRI scans taken within two weeks before and after the eight-week programme, and a participant cannot deliberately change the density of their hippocampus in the way they could bias a questionnaire. The wait-list control group was matched to the same entry criteria and showed no grey-matter changes of its own (no increase in the left hippocampus), and the two groups did not differ at baseline (e.g. pre-test left-hippocampus scores and education level). Therefore, the increase in left hippocampal grey matter in the MBSR group can be attributed to the intervention rather than to expectancy or the passage of time.

S2 · Reliability / Self-reports (quantitative comparison)： Participants completed the 39-item Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire at two fixed time points, before and after MBSR or the waiting period, and the MBSR group improved significantly on three facets — acting with awareness, observing and non-judging — while the control group did not change. As a result, the subjective experience of mindfulness could be compared across groups and time to test whether the programme had longer-term effects alongside the brain changes. Therefore, the study combines a standardised questionnaire with a standardised 8-week course (weekly 2.5-hour sessions plus one full-day session), both of which are replicable.

W1 · Generalisability： Only 16 participants completed the MBSR group (two men withdrew after the first scan) and 17 were in the wait-list control, all aged 25–55, self-referred or doctor-referred stressed but psychologically and physically healthy adults who had signed up for an MBSR course at one US university. Therefore, the brain changes may not generalise to older adults, clinical populations or people who did not themselves choose to attend a stress-reduction course.

W2 · Self-reports / interpretive limits： The FFMQ is a self-report, so the mindfulness scores depend on participants' own subjective judgements rather than any physiological measure. More importantly, the grey-matter increases correlated with neither the amount of homework practice (a mean of 22.6 hours overall) nor the change in FFMQ scores, and the authors themselves note that further research is needed with a control group receiving only the non-mindfulness-specific parts of the course (such as the social contact of the full-day session), so non-specific factors cannot yet be separated from mindfulness itself. Therefore, the claim that mindfulness practice changes the brain remains under-determined by the data.

Dement & Kleitman (1957) ★预测

S1 · Reliability / Objective measurement： The study used objective physiological measurement, making the data reliable. Sleep stage was identified from EEG brain-wave records and eye movements from EOG electrodes placed around the eyes, with the wires gathered into a single cord so participants could move freely; the same electrode placement was used for every participant every night, so the recorded information was identical in kind. Waking was triggered by a doorbell loud enough to rouse participants instantly from any stage, and the procedure produced results consistent with earlier findings (Aserinsky & Kleitman, 1955). Therefore, the key measurements do not depend on the experimenters' judgement, and the method is replicable.

S2 · Validity (controls)： Participants did not know their EEG pattern or their eye movements, so they could not tailor their dream reports to what the experimenters expected — demand characteristics were low. A dream only counted if the participant could recall specific content, with vague "I dreamt something" reports excluded, which operationalised dreaming strictly. To control forgetting, participants were woken immediately by the doorbell, and one participant who might have learned the waking pattern (DN) still reported like the others, showing no practice effect. Therefore, the dramatic difference — dreams recalled after 152 of 191 REM awakenings (79.6%) but only 11 of 160 NREM awakenings (6.9%) — is a valid estimate of the REM–dream link.

W1 · Generalisability： Only nine adults were studied — seven men and two women — with the main results coming from just five of them, all volunteers, who may have been people who dream vividly and remember dreams well (a self-selection bias). Therefore, the finding that everyone dreams on every night, and that dreams mostly occur in REM, is based on a handful of potentially untypical sleepers.

W2 · Ecological validity / Ethics： Sleeping in the laboratory is not normal sleep. Participants spent the night in a dark but unfamiliar room, attached to EEG and EOG electrodes gathered into a "ponytail" of wires, under observation, and woken repeatedly at various times during the night by a doorbell, and they had to abstain from caffeine and alcohol, which may itself have made their sleep atypical. There was also deception: participant WD was told he would only be woken in REM when in fact he was woken in both stages, so fully informed consent was impossible. Therefore, the dream–REM relationship found may partly reflect the artificial conditions rather than natural sleep.

Bandura 已考 3 次 · 配 Fagen(w24) / S&S(w25) / Perry(s26)

Both the Fagen study and the Bandura study used observation as part of their data collection techniques. This means that specific behaviours could be operationalised to improve validity (explanation). For example, in Fagen there was trunk down which meant the trunk was hanging loose and in Bandura gun play was pretending to shoot at an object in the room.

建议写的维度：① Observation 作 similarity（上句官方满分）；② Experimental nature / IV（模型类型：攻击-非攻击、男-女）；③ Quantitative data（攻击行为模仿次数 → aggressive/nonaggressive、male/female 直接比较）。高风险：experimental design 作差异 = 0（w24_13）；both used children = L1（w25_12）；多卷禁 sample。

Milgram 已考 3 次 · 配 Perry(w24) / Piliavin(s25) / Piliavin(s26)

Both the Piliavin and Milgram studies have ethical issues. Both studies broke the guideline of deception and stressed participants [explanation]. In the Milgram study, participants believed they were giving real electric shocks to a stranger who could not remember word pairs. In the Piliavin study, the participants were led to believe that the drunk or ill victim needed help.

建议写的维度：① Ethics 作 similarity（上句官方满分，s26_11）；② Stooges 作 difference（human stooge vs animated，w24_12 官方）；③ Quantitative data（最大电击电压）。高风险：ethics 作 difference 只认 Consent / harm / right to withdraw / deception——Confidentiality / Debriefing = 0 分（w24_12）；experimental designs 作差异 = 0；both volunteer samples = 0。

Piliavin 已考 2 次 · 配 Perry(s25) / Milgram(s26)

The studies used stooges in different ways (explanation). In the Piliavin study a human stooge played the ill or drunk victim and further stooges acted as models, whereas Perry used an animated (computer) stooge.

建议写的维度：① Ethics 作 similarity（与 Milgram 共用上题官方句）；② Stooges 作 difference（human victim/model stooge vs animated）；③ Observation 作 similarity（s26_11 清单明示 both used observations）；④ Quantitative data（帮助潜伏时间、按种族/受害者计的帮助人数）。高风险：sample 必答卷（s25_12）——至少各研究一项样本信息正确、且必须明确点出差异是什么才到 L4；sampling technique 不算 sample；只描述两个样本 = L2。

Perry 已考 3 次 · 配 Milgram(w24) / Piliavin(s25) / Bandura(s26)

The studies used stooges in different ways (explanation). Milgram used a human stooge that the participant met beforehand but did not see during the duration of the procedure. In the Perry study the participants 'met' the animated stooge in various rooms/scenarios until they felt the stooge was invading personal space.

建议写的维度：① Stooges 作 difference（真人 vs 动画，上句官方满分 w24_12/s25_12）；② Ethics 作 difference（VR 情境避免真实伤害，注意只认四条指南）；③ Experimental nature（VR 情境实验的 IV：oxytocin/安慰剂）。高风险：Confidentiality / Debriefing = 0 分；生物卷配对时 Perry 仅限其生物面向（m25_12）/ 仅限 oxytocin（s25_13）；use of a model 维度与 stooges 同源可互换（s26_13）。

Andrade 已考 3 次 · 配 Pozzulo(m24) / 认知研究(s25) / Baron-Cohen(w25)

The studies by Andrade and Pozzulo used different experimental designs within them. Andrade was only independent groups with participants being placed in the doodling or control group. Part of the Pozzulo study was repeated measures as all participants saw target absent and target present line-ups. Therefore, in Andrade Ps only participated in one level of the IV whilst in Pozzulo Ps participated in multiple levels of the IV (explanation).

建议写的维度：① Experimental design 作 difference（上句官方满分 s25_11，但该维度只在部分卷给分）；② Experimental nature（doodling 有无，m24_12/w25_13 官方）；③ Cognitive skills 作 difference（sustained attention vs 面孔再认/情绪识别，用准术语）；④ Quantitative data。高风险：audio vs visual task = L3 max（s25_11）；standardisation 比较 / different conclusions = L0；禁 sample 卷里 sampling technique 能否答逐字读题。

题型六 · Debate Q7 · 4 分 · individual vs situational / nature-nurture

怎么答

问的是individual vs situational（或 nature vs nurture）：两句定义 + 两个例子。

  1. 定义 individual：背官方原句（见下）。
  2. 该研究的 individual 例子（1 句，必须来自题目指定的研究）。
  3. 定义 situational：背官方原句。
  4. 该研究的 situational 例子（1 句）。
  • 循环定义不给分："situational is about the situation" = 0 分。
  • 用别的研究的例子 = 0 分。
概念官方定义（逐字背，7 份 MS 完全一致）
IndividualThe individual side refers to behaviours from factors within the person/organism (dispositional).
SituationalThe situational side refers to behaviour from factors in the external environment.
NatureNature is about behaviours being genetically encoded and with us from birth.
NurtureNurture is about the learning of behaviours through experiences/after birth.

题型七 · Application to Everyday Life Q5 · 4 分 · two applications

怎么答

问的是两条"怎么用这个研究"的建议，每条 = what + how 各 1 分。

  1. WHAT：用在哪个具体人群或场景（teachers、nurses、therapists…不能只写 people）。
  2. HOW：具体怎么做——借用研究里的方法（EEG、token system、MRI、正念课程…），不能只写术语。
  • 写"这个研究解释了为什么…"不给分——必须写"可以用它去做…"。
  • 两条不能是同一个想法（换个说法或换个人群也算重复），只给一条的分。

题型八 · Aim 研究目的 2–4 分 · "State / Outline the aim(s)"

怎么答

问的是研究想干什么——不是研究做了什么、更不是发现了什么。这类题背熟原句就是送分题。

  1. 原句都以 To investigate / To see if / To test / To identify 开头，直接默写下面对应研究的句子。
  2. 一个研究常有多条 aim：题目问几条写几条；没说数量就写最核心的两条。
  3. 多份 MS 都有同一条提示：不能照抄题目里的原词——用背好的原句就不会踩这条。
  • 写成研究结果或结论（如 "found that 65% obeyed"）= 0 分。
  • 把 aim 和 hypothesis（可检验的预测）混在一起写。

12 个研究的 Aim（背诵版）

Milgram (1963)

To investigate the level of obedience when an authority figure orders a person to administer a physical punishment to a stranger.

一条 aim。别顺手写 65%——那是结果。

Piliavin et al. (1969)

To investigate factors affecting helping behaviour on a New York subway train — specifically how the following affected help offered to a passenger who collapsed in the carriage: the type of victim (drunk or ill); the race of the victim (black or white); modelled help provided by another passenger; the number of people in the carriage (group size).

核心句 + 四个因素，四个都要列全。

Perry et al. (2015)

To investigate how oxytocin affects preferred interpersonal distance for those scoring high or low in empathy traits.

三个要素一个不能少：oxytocin / preferred interpersonal distance / 高低共情组。

Bandura, Ross & Ross (1961)

  1. To investigate whether children imitate aggression of a model in the absence of the model.
  2. To investigate whether children are more likely to imitate the behaviour of a same-sex model.

两条都围绕 imitation；"in the absence of the model" 是第一条的关键短语。

Andrade (2010)

  1. To investigate whether doodling improves our ability to pay attention to (or concentrate on) auditory information.
  2. To investigate whether doodling affects later recall of auditory information.

两条 = attention 一条 + recall 一条；只写 "helps memory" 太笼统不给分。

Baron-Cohen et al. (2001)

  1. To test adults with AS/HFA on the revised Eyes Test to see if the deficits on the original test were still seen.
  2. To see if there is a negative correlation between AQ and Eyes Test scores.
  3. To see if females score higher on the Eyes Test than males.

三条对应三组分析（临床组 / AQ 相关 / 性别差异）。

Pozzulo et al. (2011)

To explore the role of social and cognitive factors in children's identification of target faces in line-ups — specifically whether children: are less able to recognise human faces than adults; make more false positive identifications than adults when faced with target-absent line-ups versus target-present line-ups, and human faces versus cartoon characters.

social and cognitive factors 是主句关键词，两个"是否"各算一条。

Dement & Kleitman (1957)

  1. To see if dream recall occurs during REM or non-REM sleep.
  2. To see if there is a link between the estimate of dream length and the length of REM period.
  3. To see if the pattern of eye movements (vertical/horizontal) is related to dream content.
  4. To see if there is a positive correlation between the length of REM and the number of words given in a dream narrative.

四条对应四个研究问题，任选题目要求数量默写。

Fagen et al. (2014)

To investigate whether secondary positive reinforcement (SPR) could be used to train elephants to voluntarily complete a trunk wash — a behaviour that allows the elephants to be tested for a disease called TB.

trunk wash 和 TB 的关系（为了筛查结核）要带上。

Saavedra & Silverman (2002)

  1. To highlight the role of evaluative learning and disgust in the development and treatment of children's phobias.
  2. To test the efficacy (effectiveness) of imagery exposure as part of an exposure-based cognitive-behavioural treatment for a specific phobia of buttons.

evaluative learning + disgust 两个关键词在第 1 条里。

Hassett et al. (2008)

  1. To test if sex differences in children's toy preferences result from biological factors (e.g. prenatal hormone exposure) rather than through socialisation.
  2. To investigate if male and female rhesus monkeys have similar toy preferences to human infants, despite no socialisation experience with human toys.

第 2 条的 "despite no socialisation" 是猴子做被试的理由，别删。

Hölzel et al. (2011)

  1. To identify if regular participation in Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) leads to measurable neurological changes.
  2. To identify brain structures which changed as a result of an eight-week MBSR programme.

两条都以 To identify 开头；别写成 "meditation changes the brain" 这种结论句。

三、失分陷阱 TOP10（按出现频率排序，附英文原话）

TOP 1 · Q9(b) "different aims" / 只分别描述两研究

"Different aims" = L1 max; just describing what happened in each study is treated as different aims.

几乎所有 B 类卷都写了这条。对策：第一句必须直接点明相似/不同点是什么。

TOP 2 · 未明确点出异同（2025–2026 收紧）

If the similarity/difference is not explicitly stated = L2 max (B10/B13/B16/B17/B18).

读起来像两研究各描述一遍 = L1。点明句在前，例证在后。

TOP 3 · 踩题干禁区

"Do not refer to sample / questionnaires / brain measurement techniques / sampling / approaches" — 写了就是 L0 或整点不给分。

审题第一；注意禁 sample 的卷里 sampling technique 是否可答要逐字读题干（两卷口径相反）。

TOP 4 · 无解释句 / "easy to analyse" 充当解释

Description only = L3 max; "easy to analyse/compare" does not count as the explanation for quantitative data.

解释必须说比较带来了什么（direct/statistical comparisons across groups）并以 Therefore 开头。

TOP 5 · Debate 同义反复定义

"Ignore tautological definitions"; "Do not credit individual differences as a definition mark."

定义只有官方两句，逐字背；"individual differences" 放定义位 0 分、放例子位 1 分。

TOP 6 · Application 写成回顾性解释

"They must be proactive/prospective applications, not explanations/retrospective"; "help us understand X" = 0.

检查句式：开头必须是 can/could be used to do 什么。

TOP 7 · Application 人群/场景不具体

"Do not credit responses that simply state 'people / children / friends can'."

开头点名人群：nurses、teachers、therapists、toy manufacturers、police officers；只写 "stressed people" max 1。

TOP 8 · 10 分题 named issue 缺失 / no context 泛泛点

Not including the named issue = max L3 (5–6 marks); no-context points (e.g. "There was a standardised procedure meaning it could be replicated") only support low bands.

写前 30 秒先问：题目指定的议题是什么、哪两点回应它；每个论点挂具体细节（数字、器材、程序）。

TOP 9 · 研究事实张冠李戴

Milgram–Perry ethics difference only credits Consent / harm / right to withdraw / deception (Confidentiality / Debriefing = 0); Baron-Cohen ethics only three guidelines (right to withdraw / deception / privacy / debrief not in the paper); "Do not credit Fagen as a field experiment"; D&K sampling only opportunity.

另：S&S 不是 lab experiment 也不是 observation；Hassett 不是 lab experiment；混淆研究名称/术语 = L2 max（标 NBOD）。

TOP 10 · 卷专属黑名单 + 两条 application 同源

Perry: "giving oxytocin to a population"; Hassett: "anything about gender issues and mental health"; Fagen: "using SPR/rewards on animals for entertainment"; Hölzel: brain-scan applications must say MRI; "same idea but just a different population then only credit once" (REP).

术语（SPR、mindfulness、imagery exposure）不能当 how；两条只写一条 = 丢 2 分——4 分版是两栏结构，写满。

四、考前一天背诵清单（checklist）

背诵项内容（库内原文）
□ Debate 两句定义（ind-sit）"The individual side refers to behaviours from factors within the person/organism (dispositional)." / "The situational side refers to behaviour from factors in the external environment."
□ Debate 两句定义（N/N）"Nature is about behaviours being genetically encoded and with us from birth." / "Nurture is about the learning of behaviours through experiences/after birth."
□ Q9(b) L4 公式点明异同 → For example, in study A … → In study B … → Therefore / This means that …（缺 Therefore 最高 L3；缺点明最高 L2）
□ Q5 两步公式WHAT（tick）：specific population + goal；HOW（plus）：借研究方法要素的动作。自检三问：人群具体？how 是动作？两条 what 不同源？
□ Q10 三句式论点 → 该研究 2–3 个具体细节（数字/器材/程序）→ Therefore 后果句；named issue 必踩否则 max L3。
□ Milgram 最稳证据句The prods were always the same and the feedback from the learner never changed (it was recorded) → easier to replicate for comparable findings.
□ Piliavin 最稳证据句They timed how quickly people helped / how many of each race helped → compare helping between races or victim types (95% cane vs 50% drunk).
□ Perry 最稳证据句Double-blind oxytocin vs saline placebo, two counterbalanced sessions one week apart → differences cannot be explained by researcher expectations or order effects.
□ Andrade 最稳证据句Every participant heard the same mock telephone message at 227 words per minute → increases reliability / replicable.
□ Baron-Cohen 最稳证据句They counted the number of correct emotions identified from the eyes → compare performance across the four groups (AS/HFA 21.9 vs student 28.0).
□ Pozzulo 最稳证据句For every participant the instructions for the line-up identification process were the same; the cartoon targets were Dora and Diego → testable for reliability.
□ Bandura 最稳证据句Participants were rated on aggression prior to the study → more confident it was the IV (type of model) affecting the DV.
□ S&S 最稳证据句The boy rated buttons on a Feelings Thermometer so progression across therapy sessions could be compared（n=1 是最稳 weakness）。
□ Fagen 最稳证据句They counted the number of trials it took to pass the trunk wash → compare performance across the five elephants; only 5 elephants → may not represent other elephants including adult ones.
□ Hassett 最稳证据句Only 34 rhesus monkeys, some of the troop too young to be reliably identified, captive troop → findings may only apply to those in the sample / not wild monkeys.
□ Hölzel 最稳证据句Participants completed the FFMQ at two time points (before and after MBSR or control) → compare changes in all five mindfulness measures for both groups.
□ D&K 最稳证据句Dreams recalled after 152 of 191 REM awakenings (79.6%) but only 11 of 160 NREM awakenings (6.9%)；EEG/EOG 客观记录。
□ 两句 Q9(b) 官方满分范例MRI vs EEG："Hölzel's method looked at structure whereas Dement looked at (real-time) function"；Stooges："Milgram used a human stooge … Perry … the animated stooge"。
□ 优先准备D&K 与 Perry 的 Q10 从未考（很可能接着考）；Q9(b) 未考配对 4 组（Pozzulo–B-C / Hassett–D&K / Hassett–Hölzel / Fagen–S&S）；Q5 未考研究 2 个（Piliavin、Baron-Cohen）。
