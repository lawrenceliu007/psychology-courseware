题型一 · 术语定义Q1 · 1–2 分

怎么答

"Describe/Outline/Define what is meant by X"

  1. 给分结构：Outline/Define = 1 分，Detail/Example = 1 分（2 分题）。1 分题只需一句正确定义。
  2. 定义要写它是什么，不能写它"不是什么"——"Stating what it is not = 0 marks"（w24_21）。
  3. 带 [definitive] 的术语必须用准确术语名，描述性替代 = 0："the Ps were selected by replying to an advert = 0"，必须写 volunteer sampling（s24_21）。

题型二 · 核心研究情境题Q2–5 · 1–4 分

怎么答

"In the study by X (…), explain/outline/state…"——拿核心研究当载体考 RM 概念。

  1. 黄金公式：definitive 术语 + 一句具体 link 到研究细节。generic = 1 分，linked = 2 分的分层贯穿全卷（w24_22、s25_23、w25_22 多卷明示）。
  2. 数字解读题（mean/SD）：必须写比较性陈述（more spread out / older than），禁止复述数字。
  3. 注意出题人只挑原始论文有抽样信息的研究出 sampling 题："No marks for Bandura et al., Hölzel et al., Perry et al. as no information on sampling in the original paper"（s25_21 MS 原文）。

Correlation ≠ Cause（反复出现）标准答语

  • 结论题：Explain what she will conclude → positive correlation + as both variables go up together。"One variable causes the other" = 0；"tidy work makes students enjoy lessons" = 0（s25_21）
  • 能否下因果结论：Explain whether X can conclude that Y influences Z → No, a correlation does not mean causation + a third factor could be causing both（e.g. parenting）（s25_21、w25_22——至少一处 link 才满分）
  • 为何用 correlation 不用 experiment：the relationship may not be causal / unethical to manipulate the variable（m25_22）
  • 反驳他人因果结论：Explain why this conclusion is not correct → cannot say causal + could only conclude the two are related + third factor（w25_22）

  • 下结论：Any conclusion = 1，complete（含比较方向）= 2。just restating results = 0 [NAQ]（w24_21）
  • 反驳结论：the difference is very small → might not be significant / could be due to chance（s24_22）
  • longitudinal vs cross-sectional：advantage of comparing different ages at one time = quicker/cheaper/no attrition（"changes over the lifespan" = 0 [NAQ]，s25_21）

[d ✓]Analysis: the checklists and rating scales give quantitative data (frequencies, means) and the interviews give qualitative data. Mary uses triangulation — checking that the three sources agree — so she can be confident in her conclusion about the function of his doodling.

为什么这是 L5：MS 的 4 个 feature 原文逐条对应；triangulation 按 MS 原话写"sources agree → confident conclusion"（MS 特别注明这不是 reliability，别写错词）。✅ 已对照官方 ER 验证（s24）：ER 点名案例研究三大失分点——写成实验（两组对比）、只有一种方法、缺背景信息来源——本范文全部避开：三种 techniques + 家长/老师背景信息 + 零 IV/DV 语言，与 ER 描述的高分答卷完全一致。

Observational study已考 2 次：s24_22 participant / w24_22 structured

  1. Participant / non-participant——observer 怎么进入情境（server in the canteen）
  2. Overt / covert——被观察者知不知道（explicit and separate——必须单列说明）
  3. Naturalistic / controlled——基本是 naturalistic + 说明为什么
  4. Structured (behavioural categories) / unstructured——structured 要列 至少 3 个 behavioural categories + operational definitions
  • 四个维度每个都要 explicit——混在一句里只 tick 一次
  • categories 必须可观察（"being friendly" 不行，"smiling at others" 行）
🏆 满分范文（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：s24_22 · Chloë — participant observation of mealtimes at work

[a ✓]Participant observation: Chloë takes a job as a server in the staff canteen, so she takes part in the social context of mealtimes — serving and eating alongside the workers — while recording their behaviour.

[b ✓]The observation is covert: the workers are not told she is observing them, so they behave naturally at mealtimes.

[c ✓]It is naturalistic: the behaviour happens in the real workplace during real mealtimes, not a set-up situation (though the type of food served and the time of day vary naturally).

[d ✓]It is structured, using behavioural categories, e.g.: (1) eats alone in silence; (2) eats while talking to one colleague; (3) eats in a group of three or more with conversation; (4) shares food with others. Chloë unobtrusively tallies each category on a checklist kept on her phone during every mealtime (looking as if she is checking messages, so the observation stays covert).

为什么这是 L5：MS 明示四个维度必须 explicit and separate——范文每段只写一个维度；categories 全部是可观察行为（不是 "sociable" 这种模糊词）。✅ 已对照官方 ER 验证（s24）：ER 描述的高分答卷 = researcher 以员工身份混入 + 坐在参与者身边 + 手机 covert 记录 + fully operationalised categories——本范文逐条命中；且避开了 ER 点名的三个坑（坐开观察/用摄像头/加问卷）。

Experiment（lab / IMD）已考 3 次：m24_22 lab / s25_21 IMD / s25_23

  1. IV——operationalised（smells: flowers vs unpleasant；art vs music vs dance——两个具体水平）
  2. DV——operationalised + 怎么测（time asleep / quality rating / EEG / test scores）
  3. Controls / standardisation——至少 2 个具体方面（sleep location, time to bed, nature/intensity of smell, silence）
  4. Experimental design——named（independent measures: different Ps in each condition, how allocated i.e. random；repeated: counterbalancing?）
  • controls 只写 "controlled variables" 不列具体 → 没有 underline
  • IMD 卷可以补 location（field 版）替代 design（s25_21 给了 And/OR 选项）
🏆 满分范文 1 · Lab experiment（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：m24_22 · Henri — pleasant smells and daydreaming

[a ✓]IV: the smell in the room, operationalised as two conditions — participants sit in a room with a pleasant flower smell (fresh flowers placed in the room) vs a no-smell control condition (same room, no flowers).

[b ✓]DV: daydreaming, operationalised as the number of daydreams each participant reports — they press a handheld counter every time they notice themselves daydreaming while listening to a 20-minute dull audio recording (as in Andrade), plus a 5-point self-report rating of "how much did you daydream?".

[c ✓]Controls: the same quiet room for everyone, same temperature and lighting, the same nature and intensity of the flower smell for every participant in that condition, and the same dull 20-minute recording for all participants.

[d ✓]Design: independent measures — different participants in each condition, allocated randomly.

为什么这是 L5：IV/DV 都 operationalised 到"两个具体水平 + 怎么测"；controls 列了 3 个具体方面（MS 要 at least 2 explicit）；design 点名 + 写清 how allocated。

🏆 满分范文 2 · IMD experiment（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：s25_21 · Dr Miller — creative activities and sleep

[a ✓]IV: the creative activity before bed, operationalised as two conditions — an art condition (painting a picture for 30 minutes) vs a control condition (reading a magazine for 30 minutes).

[b ✓]DV: how well participants sleep, operationalised as the number of minutes asleep (recorded by an EEG / sleep-tracking watch) and a 1–10 self-report rating of sleep quality in the morning.

[c ✓]Controls: everyone sleeps in the same sleep-lab location, goes to bed at the same time (10 pm) in the same bed type, and has no caffeine from the afternoon onwards.

[d ✓]Design: independent measures — different participants in the art condition and the control condition, allocated randomly. (Field version: the activities take place in real evening classes, with participants followed home.)

为什么这是 L5：MS 的 And/OR 选项两种写法都给了——考 IMD 卷背范文 2，考 lab 卷背范文 1，骨架完全一样，只换 IV/DV 内容。

Field experiment已考 2 次：s26_21 / w25_22

  1. IV——naturally occurring in the field（ads bright vs dull coloured；sex [girls vs boys]）
  2. DV——operationalised（time spent looking at ads / time playing with toys, how measured: observe）
  3. Controls / standardisation——至少 2 个（advert size/content, time of day, train route；toy size/noisiness/familiarity）
  4. Experimental design——IMD for sex（males vs females）；两个 IV 时一个必须 repeated（toy type + counterbalancing）
  • s26_21 特殊：(b) 部转考 ethics（见题型六），但 (a) 仍禁 ethics
  • 两 IV 卷：design 必须两个都交代（sex 用 IM + toy type 用 RM）
🏆 满分范文（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：s26_21 · Gladys — field experiment on toy preferences in a nursery

[a ✓]Two IVs: (1) sex — a naturally occurring IV (girls vs boys, all children aged 4 so the two groups are similar in age); (2) toy type — a "typically girls' toy" (a doll) vs a "typically boys' toy" (a toy truck).

[b ✓]DV: interest in the toys, operationalised as the time in seconds each child spends playing with each toy, timed with a stopwatch by a nursery assistant watching during the free-play hour.

[c ✓]Controls: the two toys are matched for size, noisiness and familiarity; the observation happens at the same time each day in the same corner of the nursery.

[d ✓]Design: independent measures for sex (the girls' scores are compared with the boys' scores) and repeated measures for toy type (every child plays with both toys, order counterbalanced).

为什么这是 L5：双 IV 卷最容易丢 (d)——范文把两个 IV 的 design 分开各写一句（MS 原话：sex 必须 IM、toy type 必须 RM）。"naturally occurring IV" 这个短语本身就是 MS 给的 underline 素材。

Questionnaire已考 1 次：w24_21

  1. Question format——open / closed（+ fillers），closed 写明类型（ranked scale / yes-no / multiple choice）
  2. Examples of questions——至少 2 条具体问题（2 个 prosocial behaviour，如 sharing、playing games together）
  3. Question scoring / interpretation——numerical scoring / measures of central tendency / qualitative analysis
  4. Technique——paper & pencil 还是 online
  • 问题例子不能抄题干给的 behaviour——要自选新的
🏆 满分范文（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：w24_21 · Tanya — questionnaire for teachers on prosocial behaviours

[a ✓]Question format: mostly closed questions using a 4-point ranked scale (never / sometimes / often / always), plus one or two open questions for detail, and a filler question first ("Which class do you teach?").

[b ✓]Example questions (behaviours chosen by Tanya, not copied from the question): "How often does the child share toys or equipment with classmates? (never / sometimes / often / always)" and "How often does the child invite other children to play games together?"

[c ✓]Scoring: each answer is scored 1–4 (never = 1 … always = 4); the marks are added to give a total prosocial score per child, and the mean scores from different teachers can be compared; open answers are analysed qualitatively for examples.

[d ✓]Technique: paper and pencil — printed questionnaires handed to every teacher in the staffroom and returned anonymously to a locked box.

为什么这是 L5：四个 feature 与 interview 卡共享三件套（format / examples / scoring）+ 第 4 个换成 technique；例子是自选的新 behaviour（shares / invites），完全避开题干已给内容。

Longitudinal study已考 1 次：s26_22

  1. Tests / tasks——measures used（tasks/tests/questions 的具体描述 + 例子）
  2. Scoring——怎么打分/分析（ratings / totals / comparing scores over time with averages）
  3. Recontacting of participants——named method：post / email / phone / social media + 记录怎么维护
  4. Controls / standardisation——across testing intervals 保持一致（同一天生日周测试等）
  • "an experiment is not acceptable"——不能只是一次 test + retest 的 RM design（必须多次 + 多 measure）
  • frequency/interval 不给分（REP）——题干已给（each birthday week yearly）
🏆 满分范文（Level 5 结构 · 可直接背）真题情景：s26_22 · Sydney — longitudinal study on children's activity choices

[a ✓]Tests/tasks: within a week of each birthday, every child does the same free-choice session: the same four activities are laid out (cooking, stories, sports, maths club) and Sydney records each child's first, second and third choice, then asks two short questions about why they chose them.

[b ✓]Scoring: each activity is scored 3 (first choice), 2 (second), 1 (third); the totals for each activity are compared over time (e.g. the mean score for sports at age 6, 7 and 8), and the "why" answers are analysed qualitatively for changing themes.

[c ✓]Recontacting: Sydney keeps a record of each family's contact details (updated at every visit) and recontacts them by email or phone before each birthday to arrange the session; families who move are traced through the centre.

[d ✓]Controls/standardisation: the same activity list, same free-choice setting, same questions and same scoring system are used at every yearly session, so each year's scores are directly comparable.

为什么这是 L5：四 feature 原文全对应；注意范文没写 "every year" 之类的频率句——那是题干已给（写 = REP = 0 分）。只写"每年怎么保持一致"。
