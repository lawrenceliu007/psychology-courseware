🔴 Biological Approach（生物取向）

Hassett et al. (2008) Bio

Monkey Toy Preferences / 猴子玩具偏好
Do monkeys show gender-typed toy preferences like humans? Rhesus monkeys' interactions with wheeled vs plush toys.

Dement & Kleitman (1957) Bio

Sleep and Dreams / 睡眠与做梦
Is dreaming linked to rapid eye movements? EEG recordings showing REM sleep correlates with dream recall.

Hölzel et al. (2011) Bio

Mindfulness Brain Scans / 正念大脑扫描
How does mindfulness meditation change brain structure? MRI scans showing increased grey matter density.

🔵 Cognitive Approach（认知取向）

Andrade (2010) Cog

Doodling / 涂鸦
Does doodling improve memory? Monitoring task performance while listening to phone messages.

Baron-Cohen et al. (2001) Cog

Eyes Test (Reading the Mind) / 眼神测试
Can adults identify emotions from eye-region photos? Sex differences in theory of mind.

Pozzulo et al. (2011) Cog

Child Witnesses & Line-ups / 儿童目击者与列队指认
Are children's identifications affected by lineup type? Sequential vs simultaneous lineups.

🟠 Social Approach（社会取向）

Milgram (1963) Soc

Obedience / 服从
How far will people go in obeying authority? The classic shock generator experiment at Yale.

Perry et al. (2015) Soc

Personal Space & Oxytocin / 个人空间与催产素
Does oxytocin affect personal space preferences? Distance kept from strangers vs familiar people.

Piliavin et al. (1969) Soc

Subway Samaritans / 地铁好人
What factors influence helping behavior? Staggering victims on NYC subway trains.

🟢 Learning Approach（学习取向）

Bandura et al. (1961) Lrn

Bobo Doll Aggression / 波波玩偶攻击行为
Do children learn aggression through observation? Adult models and imitative violence.

Fagen et al. (2014) Lrn

Elephant Learning / 大象学习
Can elephants learn using operant conditioning? Training Asian elephants with SPR (successive approximations).

Saavedra & Silverman (2002) Lrn

Button Phobia / 按钮恐惧症
Can disgust-based evaluative conditioning create phobias? Linking buttons to disgusting images.
